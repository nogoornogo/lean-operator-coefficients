### Checked Construction

- [RotationExponential](../../../../../Math/LieGroups/Matrix/RotationExponential.lean)
  proves `exp(t*X) = 1 + sin(t)*X + (1-cos(t))*X^2` whenever `X^3 = -X`,
  in any real Banach algebra. Differentiating after multiplication by
  `exp(-t*X)` reduces the proof to the zero-derivative constant theorem.
  There is no truncated exponential or numerical approximation.

  to the pinned raising, lowering and Cartan matrices, and polynomial
  identities are transported by a ring homomorphism to complex matrices.
  The analytic formula proves full periods, commuting half periods and
  the quarter-turn conjugation needed for the filling.

  defines all factors through `compactExp`, so every intermediate point
  belongs to the actual candidate by construction. `hLine_torus` proves
  equality with the coordinate lines of the actual six-circle subgroup.
  `contraction i` continuously shrinks `loop i` to the constant identity
  loop while fixing both endpoints; `loop_nullhomotopic` bundles the
  resulting homotopy relation for every `i : Fin 6`.

## 6. Scope and limitations

### Endpoint And Scope

**LEAN-CHECKED:** an endpoint-fixed null-homotopy, inside
`compactIdentityComponent`, for each of its six Cartan coordinate loops.
This bounded continuation follows the actual torus milestone `checkpoint 01`.
The result is an actual `Path.Homotopy`, not a supplied topology certificate
or a finite period check.

The global compact E6 identification needs control of the fundamental
group. Contracting these loops supplies the concrete coroot side of that
comparison. It does **not** establish that every candidate-group loop is
homotopic to a torus loop. That surjectivity/generation bridge is separate.

### Implementation Boundary

1. Prove the real Banach-algebra rotation formula for `X^3 = -X`.
2. Verify the pinned 27-dimensional simple-root matrix relations and
   deduce the half-period and quarter-turn exponential identities.
3. Construct the based contraction using actual candidate exponentials:
   `exp(s*pi*H) * exp(u*pi/2*X) * exp(s*pi*H) * exp(-u*pi/2*X)`.
   At `u=0` this is the Cartan loop; at `u=1` it is constant, and
   `s=0,1` are fixed throughout.

No new dependencies, Lie integration theorem, root classification, vacuum
assumptions or amplitude assertions enter this package. Existing physical
endpoints and scope remain unchanged. Stop after the focused audit, full
build, documentation and thematic commit. If this construction requires
new global foundations, name that blocker rather than enqueueing them.

### Exit And Remaining Bridge

**Stop gate reached:** the three frozen interfaces are closed.
This is not yet a computation of the candidate fundamental group.

1. **Closed by the later [all-torus-loop package](E6_TORUS_LOOPS.md).**
   Continuous circle-cover lifts, integer endpoint fillings and ordered
   products give an actual contraction for every torus loop. The entire
   induced map `pi_1(T) -> pi_1(K)` is proved trivial, without requiring
   an abstract winding-generator classification as an extra assumption.
2. Independently, prove that this induced map is surjective for the actual
   candidate `K`. Generation of the group by exponentials is not a proof
   that its loops are generated by torus loops. Matching periods, Lie
   algebra and center is not a substitute for this topological step.

Combining that completed first statement with the still-open second one
would control all candidate loops. Simple connectedness and the intended named global
E6 identification remain open. No new global foundation is automatically
queued. The [owning topic](../../../../topics/e6-global-topology/README.md) records
the completed conditional endpoint and the extensions deferred by user direction.

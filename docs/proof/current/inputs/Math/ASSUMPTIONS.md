## Derivative Input Necessary Conditions

The [shared field connections](../Physics/FIELD_CONNECTIONS.md) accept actual
representations, selected inclusion/extraction maps and both inverse laws.
Selected extraction covariance follows; no global equivariant projector,
compactness, unitarity or semisimplicity is required. Constant equivariant
field dictionaries lift to jets with the supplied common direction action.
The derivative entry takes the exact preimage of the complete stable relation
module. Bases supply syntax, not a target-spectrum assumption. These additive
factories do not change the existing input or global-extension conditions.

The [all-order interacting presentation](../../core/Math/INTERACTING_LAGRANGIAN_COORDINATES.md)
accepts the existing finite polynomial syntax, supplied complex group action
and full-relation stability. It constructs the actual unbounded monomial
basis and proves complete generated Euler/product/derivative/divergence rows.
No finite dimension, invariant basis, row-span premise or degree cutoff is
supplied. Odd ordering and optional selected bases are noncomputable choices.
Selected-source coordinates use an invariant subspace and the full relation
preimage; no global invariant-extension hypothesis is added. The output is
an exact possibly infinite linear presentation, not a universal finite solver.

[MixedScalarInclusion](../../../../../Math/Algebra/Constructions/MixedScalarInclusion.lean)
requires only the supplied field/direction types, and a monoid with supplied
jet representations for action naturality. Scalar equation transport assumes
every embedded seed belongs to the target two-sided differential closure.
It derives full closure inclusion; it does not assume or prove a complete
finite mixed relation presentation. See the
[mixed dossier](../../core/Math/MIXED_JET_IBP.md).

The [Higgs foundation extension](../Physics/HIGGS_LAGRANGIAN_INPUT.md) proves
the generated/direct full relation equality for the explicit four-label
density. Its canonical paired contractions require finite component indices;
internal groups and their complex representations remain arbitrary. Source
semisimplicity or an invariant-complement premise is not used to prove
nonlinear equation stability. The physical specialization uses the existing
actual gauge/Lorentz representations, with their scope stated separately.

The [quotient tests](DERIVATIVE_PROHIBITION_INPUT.md) consume the existing
complex source action, stable relations and actual quotient. Scalar charge
necessity requires a proved scalar action and a nontrivial quotient; the
support-guarded iff explicitly handles zero quotients. The common-irreducible
iff instead requires semisimplicity of the actual quotient representation.
Neither requires finite dimension. Group H is supplied; local invariance
must already be accounted for when H is used only as a residual group.

Additional relations must be stable. A selected subspace must be invariant.
Global target vanishing implies selected-input prohibition only under
surjectivity of the actual coefficient restriction; the fixed-line
counterexample proves that this hypothesis is substantive. Indexed tests
assume the specified family, rather than infer a nonlinear on-shell grading,
support enumeration or finite cutoff. Zero-grade compatibility concerns the
existing graded input before EOM. No new literature premise is used.

the two S2 components, not a general Young/Schur classification.

and exterior squares and supplies the equivariant product decomposition. Its
inputs remain an arbitrary complex representation, with no basis, dimension,
topology or new literature premise.

The finite-statistics quotient extension requires a finite statistics group,
invertibility of its cardinal in the scalar field, and commutation of its
linear action with the residual action. The quotient of the span of b(g)u-u
is equivariantly equivalent to its invariant subrepresentation. An inverse
character twist treats units-valued character components. No finite dimension,
topology or general mixed Young classification is assumed or concluded.

Arbitrary-degree permutation statistics require a complex flavor representation;
no finite-dimensionality is assumed. Mixed Young projectors additionally take a
specified tableau and use TauCeti's proved rational normalization. Each image
is a residual subrepresentation equivalent to the projector-kernel quotient.

finite-standard-carrier Schur-Weyl results have not been generalized here.

The intrinsic isotypic extension uses a finite statistics group whose cardinal
is nonzero in the scalar field and a commuting residual action. Maschke proves
semisimplicity without finite-dimensional flavor. Actual distinct isotypic
components give a nonredundant equivariant direct sum and a product of residual
Homs. For complex symmetric-group statistics, pinned proved Specht completeness
and the universe-safe regular-module class dictionary give injective partition
labels and supported-partition Hom coordinates. No basis inside multiplicity
spaces or full p/y/m/j-basis enumeration is asserted. Applying this to a relation
quotient requires stability under both residual and statistics actions, stated
explicitly in `RelationStatisticsIsotypic.coefficientEquiv`.

The finite local statistics connection assumes independently supplied local
contractions/probes with normalization and completeness certificates, and genuine
local and flavor statistics representations commuting with gauge/residual
actions. It derives the coordinate input representation and pullback compatibility.
For units-valued character relations, statistics stability is proved rather than
assumed. The statistical coefficient sector is equivalent to residual Hom from
the actual input quotient and then to its isotypic block-Hom product, with explicit
extraction/reconstruction. Neither local contraction discovery nor a choice of
multiplicity-space basis is included; arbitrary flavor dimensions remain allowed.

The simple-copy coordinate extension proves existence of a multiplicity index
and simple-module copies for each intrinsic isotypic block, without a finite
multiplicity assumption. Coordinate choices are noncanonical and residual
actions may mix copies; no copywise invariance is assumed. Chosen coordinates
give equivariant conversion and scalar-field coefficient-Hom transport. Finite
local dictionary conversion requires both dictionaries' proved normalization
and completeness and preserves the reconstructed coefficient. Canonical
statistics-Hom multiplicity spaces and Li basis identifications are not claimed.
`StatisticsMultiplicityCoordinates.chosenCoefficientEquiv` constructs the actual
block presentations without an additional existence premise and refines the
residual-Hom product; `characterMultiplicityEquiv` connects the finite local
character-sector consumer. Noncanonical choices have proved conversion and
inverse laws. The sum/difference two-contraction control has proved normalization
and completeness for both dictionaries and allows arbitrary residual flavor.

## Invariant Mass Extension Protection

[InvariantMassProtection](../../../../../Physics/FieldTheory/Mass/Algebra/InvariantMassProtection.lean)
fixes an arbitrary group G, complex representations V and W, and an invariant
symmetric mass B on V. All extensions are invariant symmetric masses on V x W
whose restriction to V is B. The original radical is retained through x -> (x,0).
Individual preservation is equivalent to vanishing of the cross map on that
radical. Universal preservation is equivalent to every ambient intertwiner
V -> W.dual restricting to zero there.

An equivariant retraction onto the radical makes this equivalent to
Hom_G(radical B,W.dual)=0. Semisimplicity of V supplies the retraction and the
common-simple-constituent criterion; no separate semisimplicity of W,
finite-dimensionality, continuity or compactness is assumed. Preserved radicals
have a constructed equivariant injection. This does not assert equality of
full light spectra, preservation under arbitrary mixing of the chosen light
embedding, or UV generation of every allowed mass. Arbitrary indexed families
extending the fixed B have a pointwise criterion for future model applications.
See the [topic record](../../../../topics/invariant-mass-protection/README.md).

## Full Unitary Majorana Stabilizers And Takagi Existence

For a finite index set I and any complex diagonal coefficients c, the
[full unitary classification](../../../../topics/majorana-unitary-stabilizer/results.md)
uses the complex bilinear congruence `U.transpose * diagonal c * U = diagonal c`,
not Hermitian conjugation. Blocks are fibers of `‖c i‖`, not of equal complex
coefficients. The zero block is unitary; each positive absolute-value block is
real orthogonal after proved phase normalization. Independent signs describe
only the distinct nonzero one-dimensional blocks, not general degenerate mixing.

The [Takagi extension](../../../../topics/majorana-congruence-interface/existence.md)
proves, for every finite complex matrix M, that symmetry is equivalent to
existence of unitary U and real nonnegative m with
`U.transpose * M * U = diagonal m` (with complex scalar coercion).
No nondegeneracy, distinctness, nonempty index or external diagonalization
premise is required. The proof uses proved finite-dimensional real spectral
theory inside Lean. `FlavorMajoranaCertificate.ofSymmetric` constructs the
physical input, and `fromDiagonal_surjective` proves complete parametrization
of symmetric matrices. Thus the stabilizer classification applies to arbitrary
finite symmetric forms, not only supplied diagonalization certificates.

The [congruence interface](../../../../topics/majorana-congruence-interface/README.md)
proves that, for a fixed diagonal target and certificate P, all certificates
are uniquely `P * S` with S in the target stabilizer. This is a bijection of
types, not a group structure on certificates. Stabilizer and residual Hom
equivalences include both inverses and a nonzero-existence criterion; consumer
modules may have arbitrary dimension. Finite dimension is required for the
matrix carrying the Majorana form. No sorted-mass uniqueness theorem,
continuously chosen normalization, topological group equivalence, simultaneous
Yukawa classification, UV matching or observed flavor data is asserted.

## Semantic Euler Naturality

[Semantic descent and naturality](../../core/Math/EULER_NATURALITY.md) use the
actual unbounded mixed ordinary-jet algebra and left graded derivatives.
Equal evaluated finite polynomial densities give equal Euler families.
The chain rule assumes a parity-preserving linear jet substitution induced by
an algebra map, its base-field map and compatible constant derivative/direction
covariance. Naturality does not require invertibility; deriving Euler-span
stability from density invariance uses an invertible base-field map.
`StandardDensityAction.model` constructs all action-side data from three group
representations and requires only evaluated invariance per density. Directions
are finite; no fixed field degree or jet order is imposed. This theorem uses
no geometric variational-bicomplex premise, analytic integral or new axiom.

      equiv Pi r : Fin(min(h,d)+1), Sym^(h+d-2r) H.

The finite dependent product has the constituent SU2 actions and represents
the finite direct sum. `degree_eq` proves equality with the implemented
natural degree (h-r)+(d-r). The inverse identities and intertwining property

actual defining doublet, not an unspecified representation with matching
characters. There is no degree cutoff or unproved decomposition parameter.

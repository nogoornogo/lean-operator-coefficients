## 2. Inputs and conclusion

### Checked Statements

Write `k = compactMatrixLieAlgebra`, `N = compactNormalizer`, and
`N0 = compactIdentityComponent`, as in the
[previous audit](E6_IDENTITY_COMPONENT.md).
[CompactLocalLog](../../../../../Math/LieGroups/E6/Minuscule/CompactLocalLog.lean) proves:

```text
For all sufficiently small matrices X:
  exp X is in N  <->  X is in k.

For all g in N sufficiently close to 1:
  compactLocalLog g = log g is in k,
  exp (compactLocalLog g) = g.

N0 is open in N.
Subgroup.closure {g in N | exists X in k, exp X = g} = N0.
```

Here `Subgroup.closure` means **algebraic subgroup generation**, not
topological closure. It expresses generation by finite products and
inverses; it does not assert that every element is a single exponential.

`exists_exp_criterion_radius` and `exists_localLog_radius` provide positive
neighborhood radii. Their existence is proved from the analytic APIs, not
assumed as a certificate. No numerical radius is calculated. Matrix
identities use the actual subgroup inclusions, not an abstract supplied
realization or a renamed compact E6 carrier.

## 4. Proof structure

### Proof Structure

1. [LieNormalizerLocalLog](../../../../../Math/LieGroups/Matrix/LieNormalizerLocalLog.lean)
   proves that the convergent operator logarithm preserves any closed
   invariant real submodule. All powers and partial sums preserve the
   submodule, and closedness preserves the limit. The local identity
   `log (exp T) = T` then recovers invariance under T from
   invariance under `exp T`, for small T. Apply this to the continuous
   commutator operator and TauCeti's exponential-conjugation identity to
   recover Lie normalization from conjugation by `exp X` near zero.
2. [SpecialUnitaryLocalLog](../../../../../Math/LieGroups/SpecialUnitary/SpecialUnitaryLocalLog.lean)
   proves the local SU criterion. Unitarity equates `exp (star X)` with
   `exp (-X)`; the local logarithm makes their exponents equal. Determinant
   one gives `exp (trace X) = 1`; the scalar local logarithm forces trace
   zero. This removes periodic ambiguities only in a genuine neighborhood.
3. [MatrixLieGroupLocalLog](../../../../../Math/LieGroups/Matrix/MatrixLieGroupLocalLog.lean)
   combines these arguments for any real matrix Lie subalgebra. The
   ambient logarithm tends to zero at the group identity, so the local
   exponential criterion applies to it. Normalization, skewness and trace
   zero are derived, not added as hypotheses on the logarithm.
4. E6's previously checked exact Lie-normalizer bound places this logarithm
   in k. Local exponential representation puts an identity neighborhood
   inside N0, making N0 open. The algebraically generated exponential
   subgroup contains that neighborhood, is open and closed, and contains
   the connected component. The opposite containment follows from E6-0h's
   continuous exponential lines, giving equality.

The prior periodic scalar `X = (2 pi i) I` remains a counterexample to an
unrestricted time-one converse. The persistent audit reuses it to prove
`no_global_exp_converse`. No local statement is silently promoted to a
global logarithm or a globally injective exponential.

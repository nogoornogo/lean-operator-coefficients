## Additional Bounded Operator Classifications

### Dimension-Six Delta L = 1

The [complete endpoint](../../../../../topics/delta-l-one-completeness/results.md) starts
from invariant exterior four-field coefficients on all ten signed SM species,
with lepton degree +1, not an assumed four-family support condition.
Hypercharge, color triality and Weyl constraints derive support on duql, qque,
qqql and duue and baryon degree +1. The resulting linear equivalence with the
explicit residual-Hom product has both inverse identities and a nonzero
criterion. Residual groups and complex multiplicities are arbitrary.

The qqql factor retains finite exchange equations among four functionals;
these are not four unconstrained coordinates. The solved duue factor is
unrestricted, not an extra symmetric-up-flavor space. Actual-field transport
requires species-wise equivariant identifications. Degree -1 is related
semilinearly only after supplying an equivariant species-reversing field
involution; unrelated negative-species representations need not admit it.
Neutral scalar multiplicities give four independent coefficients. The scope
excludes derivatives, other dimensions and extra sterile-neutrino fields.

### Specified Six-Quark Delta B = 2

The [physical-sector classification](../../../../../topics/delta-b-two-model-interface/flavor-hom.md)
uses arbitrary complex up/down multiplicities, independent actual-field
identifications and the single six-right-quark pattern of degree (2,4).
Its inverse exterior lift and surjectivity identify the sector with a residual
Hom to 25 local color/Weyl coordinates subject to four typed within-species
exchange constraints. Up and down carriers need not coincide. The output is
neither 25 free coefficients nor universally a two-dimensional space.
The two-probe equivalence and dimension 2 hold for trivial one-generation
multiplicities with trivial residual group. Other six-quark chirality patterns
and UV matching are outside this bounded endpoint.

The [extended Li local certificate](../../../applications/Physics/LI_SIX_QUARK_BASIS.md) supplies 25 independent
literal orbit contractions from the ten recovered Young seeds plus a separately
identified alternating-down / symmetric-up extension. Its two-sided rational
coordinate certificate, transported typed exchange conditions and actual-field
equivalence are checked in Lean. The first ten seed orbits cannot span the added
local component, as proved by a separating linear probe. A determinant flavor
tensor gives a nonzero physical witness for trivial residual symmetry with
up carrier C and down carrier C^4. Scalar specialization still has dimension two
and agrees with the established probes; 25 local entries are not free flavor maps.
The comparison uses an explicit epsilon dictionary and primitive-coordinate
normalization, not a four-component C-matrix or hook-length normalization claim.
It neither proves a full eleven-factor Schur decomposition nor diagnoses every
version of the paper or its software. No dimension-at-most-three assumption is
added to obtain the general certificate. See the
[topic](../../../../../topics/li-six-quark-basis/README.md) for verification.

The [printed-list flavor boundary](../../../applications/Physics/LI_SIX_QUARK_FLAVOR_BOUNDARY.md) additionally
proves that the full printed orbit span is the probe kernel, of local dimension
24. For arbitrary residual H the missing probe is symmetric-up and
alternating-down, and descends to a functional on Sym² U tensor Λ⁴ D with
an exact evaluation identity and vanishing equivalence. When down finrank
is at most three, it vanishes, yielding a two-sided linear equivalence from
the physical ModelSector (arbitrary U, finite-dimensional D of dimension ≤3) to the statistics- and
H-constrained Hom with values in the printed span. This bound is a sufficient
specialization, not an added hypothesis of the general 25-coordinate theorem.
No full eleven-factor Schur or independent residual-Hom quotient classification
is asserted by the descent alone.

### Weinberg Delta L = 2 And Majorana Forms

The [LLHH classification](../../../../../topics/delta-l-two-weinberg/results.md) uses
two alternating leptons and two symmetric Higgs inputs, equivalently an input
of exterior-square leptons tensor symmetric-square Higgs, not four alternating
inputs. One Higgs doublet has trivial residual action. Arbitrary lepton
multiplicity F gives Hom_H(Sym^2 F,C); no finite-dimensionality, compactness or
self-duality premise is needed. Equivariant identifications with actual fields
are supplied inputs. At a supplied neutral VEV of amplitude v, the Majorana
form is v^2 times the residual coefficient; invertibility requires v != 0.
A zero VEV yields zero mass. This is not a classification of all Delta L = 2
operators, general Higgs sectors, or a UV seesaw matching theorem.

The [relation-quotient adapter](../../../../../../Physics/StandardModel/Operators/Weinberg/WeinbergRelationCompleteness.lean)
also classifies the actual `WeinbergModelSector` by residual Hom from the
explicit exchange-generated input quotient. Its comparison with the existing
symmetric-square Hom equivalence commutes. This is a Hom-space equivalence;
no new input-carrier identification or additional Higgs/UV assumption is made.
See the [general presentation dossier](../../../core/Math/RELATION_COMPLETENESS.md).

### CLFV Dipoles And Admissible Generation

The [integrated dipole endpoint](../../../../../topics/clfv-dipole-completeness/integration.md)

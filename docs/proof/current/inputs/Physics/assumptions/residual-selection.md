## Residual selection criteria

The [finite-multiplicity extension](../../../applications/Physics/FLAVORED_CYCLIC_SELECTION.md) supplies positive
cyclic order, five independent finite index types and diagonal charge maps.
Dimensions may differ or vanish. Exact neutral independent-coordinate spaces
classify LLHH and all four signed dimension-six families, preserving QQUE
symmetry and QQQL's symmetric/alternating/mixed constraints. DUUE's ordered up
pair is the proved exchange-solved coordinate space. The full signed exterior
input is equivalent to their product; allowance, prohibition and finranks are
necessary and sufficient. Signed conjugate charges are opposite; no physical
dual identification is silently assumed. Singleton scalar criteria are recovered.
Higgs remains residual-neutral. This is coefficient existence, not anomaly,
Yukawa, quantum or UV-generation compatibility; general six-quark flavor
selection and arbitrary-nonabelian executable Hom computation are not included.

The [specified six-quark extension](../../../applications/Physics/DELTA_B_TWO_SELECTION_CRITERIA.md) fixes
both up/down flavor carriers to ℂ and proves allowance iff their degree-weighted
character is trivial. Under neutrality the physical sector has two coordinates;
individual characters need not be trivial. Cyclic specialization requires
positive natural order and gives `2u+4d=0`. Actual fields require the existing
equivariant identifications. This does not extend to arbitrary multiplicities,
other six-quark patterns or quantum/anomaly/Yukawa compatibility claims.

The [selection dossier](../../../applications/Physics/RESIDUAL_SELECTION_CRITERIA.md) records exact
zero/nonzero transport through the four existing completeness equivalences.
Any finite-dimensional hypotheses retained in the original named selection
adapters are local to those adapters; the core six-quark physical/Hom bridge
now has a basis-free arbitrary-multiplicity inverse. The cyclic charge iff additionally fixes one-generation
complex carriers, positive cyclic order and a residual-neutral Higgs. It proves
coefficient existence, not UV generation, anomaly consistency or quantum
exactness. The signed-field dictionary uses opposite charges for conjugates.

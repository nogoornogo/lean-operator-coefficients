## Flavor Stabilizer Inputs

The [flavor construction](../../../../../topics/flavor-stabilizer/results.md) starts from
a supplied gauge representation and decomposition model for the finite-dimensional
unitary centralizer. Couplings, scalar actions, VEVs and bare forms are additional
specified data, not derived interactions. Its general family-fixing subgroup
allows arbitrary groups and families without finite-dimensionality or continuity.
For one coupling Y and VEV v, `Stab(Y) ∩ Stab(v)` is contained in `Stab(Y(v))`.
Equality with `Stab(Y(v)) ∩ Stab(v)` holds exactly when evaluation at v detects
every nonzero coupling-orbit defect among vacuum-fixing candidates. The proved
zero-VEV control shows that mass symmetry can be strictly larger. An unbroken
gauge stabilizer does not automatically replace the independently supplied
residual/flavor group in operator completeness.

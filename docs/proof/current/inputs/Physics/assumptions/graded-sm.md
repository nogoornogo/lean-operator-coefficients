constructed-quotient theorem with all ten signed fermion and both Higgs/gauge-dual
species. Flavor and scalar modules and their residual actions remain independent
and arbitrary. Actual field actions and nonzero-baryon support are identified by
proved maps, not assumed coefficient dictionaries. For every natural mixed
degree the original BNV input is equivalent to the product of residual Hom on
`((L_c)_G ⊗ M_c) / R_exchange`.

The generic factors in degrees `(4,0)` and `(4,1)` are linearly equivalent to the
existing explicit six/seven factors. Overall extraction/reconstruction commute
with the old bounded-seven theorem, and both inverse laws and zero/nonzero iff
are proved. The explicit-coordinate comparison still stops at seven; arbitrary
degrees have the general quotient interface, not newly enumerated bases.
No finite-dimensionality, compactness, semisimplicity, external generator or
spanning certificate is a premise of the general theorem. It does not assert
arbitrary-H decidability, a finite all-degree cutoff or inevitable BNV.

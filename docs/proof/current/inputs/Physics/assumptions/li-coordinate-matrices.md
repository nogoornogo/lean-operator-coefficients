## Li Printed Coordinate Matrices

The [matrix certificates](../../../applications/Physics/LI_BASIS_MATRICES.md) verify invertibility of G22
(4.38) and rank four with the specified first four rows for (4.41)/(4.44).
Inputs are transcribed finite complex coordinate data. A supplied target
linear equivalence permits coordinate-basis transfer, but neither the source
operator identities nor a differential/EOM quotient is inferred. These are
not additional EFT-sector completeness results.

Field, mass, species, operator-sector and physical-interpretation inputs live here. Algebraic foundations and model-specific realizations are linked separately; stronger wrapper inputs are not universal assumptions.

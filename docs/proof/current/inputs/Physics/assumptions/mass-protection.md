## UV-Generated Yukawa Protection And Exact Radicals

The [vacuum-family theorem](../../../../../topics/yukawa-vacuum-protection/README.md)
takes actual UV-invariant Yukawa couplings, a supplied residual-fixed vacuum,
a bare mass and an explicit extension of the old mass. Protection is equivalent
to vanishing of the total bare-plus-Yukawa cross term on the old radical.
It does not assume or establish that every residual-invariant mass is generated.

For a linear parameter family, the
[protection-kernel theorem](../../../../../topics/yukawa-protection-kernel/README.md)
expresses the locus as T(p) = -b. It is inhabited exactly when -b lies in the
range of T, and is a translated kernel once a solution is supplied.
The [exact-radical theorem](../../../../../topics/yukawa-exact-radical/README.md)
requires containment of a specified subspace L in the radical; equality holds
exactly when the induced quotient form is nondegenerate. A quotient
representation additionally requires stability of L. These abstract criteria
require neither finite-dimensionality nor semisimplicity and do not settle the
concrete SU(6) full-coupling dimension claim below.


## Algebraic Common-Copy Kernel Obstruction

`AlgebraicCommonCopyKernel.exists_common_split` takes an arbitrary group H,
semisimple source representation s, three actual intertwiners to a common
finite-dimensional target t, and positive source-minus-target finrank. No H
topology, compactness, continuity, target semisimplicity or metric invariance
is assumed. Actual simple kernel submodules and equivariant retractions are
derived; physical qque exterior existence uses the original kernels.
The [SU(5) consumer](../../../../../topics/su5-matter-mass-protection/algebraic-copy-result.md)
retains its actual common-copy matter and finite-dimensional complex vector-space
interfaces after the pure-carrier relaxation, derives species maps from arbitrary invariant symmetric B,
and proves original participating-range membership in B's radical. This is a
sufficient nonzero coefficient mechanism, not a generated Wilson coefficient
or full-light/all-BNV protection theorem.

## Model-Independent Actual-Field Physical Bridge

[The physical common-copy theorem](../../../../../topics/su5-matter-mass-protection/model-independent-result.md)
takes any actual SM-cover × H field representation and an invariant symmetric
mass. Its `Realization` supplies three injective full-copy species embeddings
and exact mass commuting squares through actual dual-field maps, with common
finite-dimensional S/T. These structural inputs imply, rather than assume,
actual kernel-range membership in the mass radical. Source semisimplicity and
dim S > dim T derive physical SM/H/Lorentz-invariant nonzero qque exterior
coefficients on the participating ranges. No unified-group carrier, topology,
metric or target semisimplicity is supplied. Actual restricted actions and
right-handed dual conventions are proved in the shared interface. H acts
trivially on the Weyl spinor factors, as in the existing physical construction.

`Model` requires the realization to be derived for every invariant symmetric
mass on the fixed field/action model. This is a model-specific classification obligation, not an
automatic consequence of group embedding or dimension. The generic
`speciesOfDirac` constructor consumes an exact normal-form equality and actual
species diagrams, with finite-dimensional actual Dirac target for biduality;
the shared theorem does not assume the full mass is Dirac or require finite F.
The SU(5) adapter proves this obligation with its existing arbitrary-B normal
form. Whole-light extension, global quotient descent, UV copy-action derivation,
matching, rates and all-BNV protection remain outside the conclusion.

## Independent Species Surplus

The [species-dependent bridge](../../../applications/Physics/SPECIES_COPY_SURPLUS_QQUE.md)
uses independent finite residual copy sources/targets for Q,uc,ec, arbitrary H,
semisimple sources, one finite irreducible R and strict Hom surplus in each
species. Full embeddings and original-mass commuting squares are structural
inputs; light support, actual restricted action and the physical exterior
coefficient are derived. No target semisimplicity/topology is needed. F may be
arbitrary. Spinor residual action is trivial; right-handed fields are duals of
actual left-handed ranges. The endpoint is on participating ranges.

The all-B constructor accepts actual sector decompositions and NoPairings to
the source and complementary sectors. Finite gauge irreducibility derives
allowed-pairing factorization, then arbitrary B derives copy masses and squares.
No whole-mass Dirac form or selected light certificate is assumed. The remaining
sector may have Majorana masses. Full UV branching, quotient descent, generated
coefficients and rates remain outside this conditional theorem. Existing common
S/T statements remain unchanged; failing surplus does not imply prohibition.

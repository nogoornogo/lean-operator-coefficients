## What The Conclusions Do And Do Not Say

The Abelian endpoint supplies **supported neutral signatures**, not a
full-SM-gauge tensor on every possible radical.

The torus-only non-Abelian endpoints are stronger than support alone: they
construct nonzero intertwining maps to the trivial representation, namely

```text
Hom_H((Q_0 tensor Q_3) tensor (u_8* tensor e_14*), C) != 0
Hom_H((d_9* tensor u_8*) tensor (Q_2 tensor L_13), C) != 0.
```

The compact continuous endpoint proves the first line (qque). The second line
is retained under the finite pair theorem's additional down-target hypotheses.

Subscripts are SM weights, not generations. All factors come from actual
light spaces; the starred factors are contragredients of left-chiral sectors.
The selected weights have zero total torus weight. The direct Lean conclusion
is an **invariant functional**, equivalently an invariant element in the
dual tensor space, not a theorem phrased as a vector in the unstarred product.

Those torus-only endpoints do not themselves supply a fully SM-equivariant
lift or Lorentz/Grassmann nonvanishing on an arbitrary radical.

**Routes A/B and the exact-spectrum, virtual-index, shared-positive and
pairwise-positive, automatic species, balanced-extension and global vectorlike
entries prove the stronger algebraic conclusion** under
their respective input packages. For actual participating fields Q, U and E_right,
let `V = (Q tensor S_L) directSum (U tensor S_R) directSum (E_right tensor S_R)`.
They construct a qque-originating coefficient whose alternating descent gives

```text
Hom_((SM-cover x H) x SL(2,C))(ExteriorPower 4 V, C_trivial) != 0.
```

Thus fermionic alternation does not kill this coefficient, including the
one-generation control. It lives on actual subspaces of K and K.dual with
spinors, not solely on an unrelated auxiliary multiplicity model. It is not
an assertion about every flavor coefficient or the whole UV field space.

None of the selection-rule/exterior-existence endpoints described above proves
generation of a Wilson coefficient. The separate
[tree-matching results](../../../../../topics/sun-conjugate-matching/README.md) construct
nonzero exchange coefficients for their specified normalized quadratic actions.
The [light-radical connection](../../../../../topics/light-matching-coherence/README.md)
recovers the SU(5) exchange on actual zero-mass radical fields. Its
[joint species extension](../../../../../topics/light-matching-coherence/joint-species.md)
restricts those same currents to the canonical full Q/up/e images, derives
SM x H invariance, and reconstructs the generated nonzero exterior coefficient
from its residual Hom. H is arbitrary with the existing ordinary unitary
centralizer action; n > 0 and g,v != 0 remain inputs. This covers the retained
ten-sector exchange, not arbitrary fermion masses or all UV interactions.
No endpoint proves a nonzero physical decay amplitude or
finite proton lifetime. The exterior functional is an algebraic
object, not a constructed interacting quantum field theory. The extra SM
inputs remain essential; none of these exterior entries upgrades all torus-only
sources or model-specific wrappers without checking them.

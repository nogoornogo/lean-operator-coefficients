## Physical Interpretation And Exclusions

The formalization stops at exact algebraic coefficient and symmetry results.
RGE evolution, nonperturbative QCD, hadronic matrix elements and lattice
calculations are outside its scope. Allowed coefficients alone do not establish
interaction generation, decay amplitudes or lifetimes.

The residual H action is an ordinary complex-linear internal action. Quantum
anomaly consistency, spacetime/R symmetries, generalized/non-invertible
symmetries, and noncommuting residual actions are not supplied by these theorems.
The [continuous anomaly cross-check](../../../applications/Physics/CONTINUOUS_ANOMALY_CROSSCHECK.md) is auxiliary,
not a proof excluding finite residual symmetries. Exact quantum symmetry is
an external interpretation, not a conclusion from the representation alone.

Realistic masses, CKM mixing and a dynamically selected VEV are neither common
inputs nor proved outputs. The specified SU(5) VEV stabilizer above is proved,
but is not a dynamical vacuum-selection result or a universal exact-SM vacuum
claim for the source/no-go models. Scalar-family
wrappers take a suitable fixed VEV as input. The [audited UV example](../../GUT/SM_VACUUM_STABILIZER.md)
has extra stabilizers. Additional gauge factors are not part of the retained
SU(6) geometry, but are not excluded abstractly by the arbitrary-G core;
such a source must still supply all actual conjugation/mass/target inputs.

The separate [self-duality chirality obstruction](../../../applications/Physics/SELF_DUAL_CHIRALITY.md)
is a different theorem, not a proton-amplitude result. No theorem here states
that every GUT necessarily causes proton decay. See [SCOPE.md](../../../applications/GUT/SCOPE.md).

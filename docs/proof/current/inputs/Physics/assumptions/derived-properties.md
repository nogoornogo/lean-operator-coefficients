# Physics Assumptions: Derived Properties, Not Extra Assumptions

[Ledger index](../ASSUMPTIONS.md). These are theorem-specific inputs, not a
universal hypothesis package. Recorded checks retain their topic checkpoints.

## Derived Properties, Not Extra Assumptions

- Abelian profile equality `P_Q = P_up = P_electron`; the fifteen-weight
  pair theorem additionally derives `P_down = P_lepton`.
- The corresponding equalities of H characters in both non-Abelian routes.
- In the fifteen-weight pair theorem, down/lepton compatibility from the first
  conjugation: it also maps weight
  10 to weight 13. A third ambient element is unnecessary with the retained
  species-uniform local target.
- Actual light support; in the finite and compact-continuous routes, common nonzero irreducible
  split quotients of the actual light sectors. Neither these projections
  nor nonzero internal coefficients are input certificates.
- Preservation of the light space by the residual symmetry and cancellation
  of the massive quotient in the dual index.
- In route B, irreducibility of the concrete SM gauge representations, their actual
  evaluation images in K/K.dual, and SM x H equivariant identifications and
  splittings. These are not supplied as a decomposition certificate.
- In routes A/B and the virtual, shared-positive, pairwise-positive and automatic exterior
  entries, the required exchange-symmetric residual
  coefficient and nonvanishing after alternation; neither is an extra input.

No hypothesis states that a particular decay channel or coupling is nonzero.
Such a premise was removed by narrowing the conclusion to selection rules,
not by proving dynamics without it.

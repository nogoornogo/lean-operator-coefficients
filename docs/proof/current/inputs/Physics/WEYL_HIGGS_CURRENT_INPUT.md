coefficients on the entire actual mixed grade (2,4,1). The normalized

generated/component extraction are proved without rank, moment,
spanning or invariant-extension assumptions.

[WeylHiggsInput](../../../../../Physics/FieldTheory/Jets/Weyl/Higgs/WeylHiggsInput.lean)

exterior/polynomial jet algebra. It fixes the global covering-SM group
times SL(2,C), integer hypercharges (+6,-6) and (+3,-3), ordinary symmetric
derivatives and signature (+---). Formal conjugates remain independent
complex generators; no star or Hermitian real dimension is supplied.

full unbounded two-sided differential closure. IBP is the linear range of
all divergences, not an ideal. Selection takes the full relation preimage
on the entire grade. The
[generated/component comparison](../../../../../Physics/FieldTheory/Jets/Weyl/Higgs/WeylHiggsRelations.lean)
identifies the union of computed Euler families and proves both coefficient
transports, preserving selected evaluations.

to one. Nonzero full-quotient support follows. The independent rank-55
diagnostic model is not a theorem premise or a proved full row presentation.

# Direction Actions And Scalar Equation/IBP Sources

## Direction Symbols And Actual Jets

The ordinary derivative-parameter module is `P = I →₀ ℂ`. Its symmetric
algebra has polynomial coordinates `Symbols I = ℂ[pᵢ]`. No factorials enter
the monomial/multi-index correspondence. `JetDirections.presentation` gives
an inverse linear equivalence between the actual jet module and a finite
sum of copies of this symbol algebra, one for each field component.
`SymmetricPolynomialPower.equiv` identifies `Symʳ(P)` with homogeneous
symbol polynomials. `orderEmbedding` embeds this order piece in the actual
jets of any specified component, is injective and commutes with direction
changes on arbitrary symmetric products.

Every linear direction map extends by polynomial substitution. Its lift to
actual jets is functorial and preserves order. On generators it satisfies

$$
T D_i v=\sum_j c_{ji}D_j T v,
\qquad c_{ji}=(T_P e_i)_j.
$$

Finite directions are required for the displayed coordinate sum. Component
and direction transformations commute; `combined` supplies their actual joint
representation. A two-direction shear at order two proves the binomial
coefficient `2t`, fixing normalization and matrix orientation.

`JetFunctor` lifts generator representations to the actual scalar polynomial
algebra and to the mixed exterior/polynomial algebra of the
[A/B result](MIXED_JET_IBP.md). Order preservation implies scalar and joint
mixed-grade preservation. The actual source embedding
`Λⁿ J_F ⊗ Symᵐ J_B → A` intertwines these actions; its derivative-grade
restriction remains a representation equivalence in both directions.
There is no new global direct-sum decomposition claim for the mixed algebra.

## Transforming Currents And IBP

For a derivative-graded family `V`, currents now carry the direction action:

$$
J_d=P\otimes V_d,\qquad
\mathrm{div}(e_i\otimes v)=D_i v.
$$

The current action is the tensor product of the direction and source actions.
The actual scalar and mixed derivatives satisfy the covariance above, so
divergence is equivariant and its range is stable. `relations_eq` proves that
this tensor-current range equals the earlier finitely supported current
range. No relation is added by changing the current presentation.

`CovariantJetIBP.coefficientEquiv` gives inverse equivariant coefficient
descent, with evaluation formulas in both directions. `DirectionalJetIBP`
instantiates this with the actual scalar/mixed derivatives and source actions.
Groups are arbitrary; no compactness, averaging, semisimplicity or invariant
exactness is used. The IBP relation remains a linear submodule.

## An Equation And All Descendants

For a specified constant-coefficient symbol `q ∈ ℂ[pᵢ]`, define the generator
relation submodule by the span of

$$
\{\,[p q]_s\mid s\text{ a component},\ p\in\mathbb C[p_i],\}.
$$

Here `[p q]ₛ = ofSymbols s (p*q)` is a linear combination of actual higher
jets. Thus every derivative descendant is present. The quotient generator
module `Q` has induced, pairwise commuting shifts. Its free scalar algebra
is `Sym(Q)`; the induced derivatives satisfy the ordinary Leibniz rule.

`projection` maps the existing scalar polynomial algebra onto `Sym(Q)`.
Its kernel is exactly the ideal generated by the linear images of the
generator relations. `presentationEquiv` proves the inverse algebra
equivalence between this EOM-ideal quotient and the free algebra on `Q`.
It also proves surjectivity and intertwining of the actual derivatives.
Ideal stability under differentiation is derived from these facts.

For a direction representation preserving `q`, the generator relations,
EOM ideal, quotient algebra and derivatives are covariant. The generic
symbol-invariance hypothesis is explicit. The
[four-dimensional specialization](../Physics/LORENTZ_SCALAR_EOM_IBP.md)
proves it for the actual massless scalar symbol and SL(2,ℂ) action.

## EOM And IBP Together

The on-shell fixed-degree space is the image of the existing scalar grade
under `projection`. Every image is finite-dimensional for finite components
and directions. Products, derivatives and actions are taken in the actual
quotient algebra; no global decomposition of it is asserted here.

Let `π_d` be the surjective map from an off-shell grade to its on-shell image.
Actual differentiation intertwines `π`, so the on-shell divergence range is
exactly the image of the off-shell divergence range. Consequently

$$
\ker(\pi_{d+1})+R_{\mathrm{IBP}}
=\pi_{d+1}^{-1}(R_{\mathrm{IBP,on}}).
$$

The first kernel is precisely the intersection of the actual EOM ideal with
the off-shell grade. `combinedQuotientEquiv` proves inverse source transport
between the two quotient constructions; `combinedRepresentationEquiv` proves
that this equivalence intertwines the actual actions. Coefficient transport
and equivariant on-shell IBP descent follow. The EOM ideal and IBP range have
different roles: the latter is never ideal-closed.

## Complete Regression And Boundaries

The complete one-direction quadratic consumer reuses the full scalar source
spanned by `(φ′)²` and `φφ″`. For the equation `φ″=0` and every descendant,
`φφ″` belongs to the EOM kernel. The previously proved divergence relation
then kills `(φ′)²`. The combined relation is the entire source; both quotient
presentations are zero and the on-shell IBP quotient has dimension zero.
This is a complete source/relations proof, not a supplied matrix.

`symbolProbe` evaluates derivative symbols at a zero of `q`. It descends to
the quotient generators and detects retained jets. The physical consumer uses
this to prove that four-dimensional massless EOM does not truncate higher jets.

The equation package is scalar and algebraic. Massive/interacting or Weyl EOM,
gauge-covariant derivatives, Bianchi relations, integrated actions, amplitude
equivalence and general EFT counting are outside this result.

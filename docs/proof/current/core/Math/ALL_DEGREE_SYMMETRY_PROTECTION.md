# Coefficient-Space Symmetry-Extension Criterion and All-Degree Charge Selection Rules

The adopted names are **coefficient-space symmetry-extension criterion** for the
necessary-and-sufficient restriction-surjectivity criterion and
**all-degree charge selection rules** for the sufficient character obstruction. They have
different logical strength. Lean declarations and file paths are unchanged.

This dossier records derivative-free algebraic coefficient protection for
supplied group actions and selected content. It does not restrict the question
to the four earlier model sectors and does not assert general spurion saturation.

The [result map](../../../../topics/all-degree-symmetry-protection/results.md)
identifies the verified declarations, the
[action and charge interfaces](../../../../topics/all-degree-symmetry-protection/interfaces.md)
state the hypotheses, and the
[verification record](../../../../topics/all-degree-symmetry-protection/verification.md)
records builds and strict audits.

Natural restriction is surjective exactly when every smaller-group invariant
coefficient is invariant under the larger group. Existing graded completeness
transports this criterion to mixed exterior/symmetric coefficient spaces.
Both the all-degree product and finite-support formulation are treated;
quantification over every finite degree bound is equivalent to all degrees.

Actual scalar actions descend through local coinvariants and statistics
relations. Nontrivial total phase forces a coefficient to vanish. Arbitrary
abelian vector charges, cyclic charges and an algebraic circle-character
specialization supply selection rules. Neutrality is necessary, not sufficient.
Flavor claims require a supplied flavor action or selector; one LFV example
cannot stand for all flavor changes.

Polynomial covariant evaluation lands in the fixed space of the actual
stabilizer. The original examples cover trivial targets and the nonzero scalar
weight-one example. The separate [finite-group theorem](FINITE_GROUP_SPURION_EVALUATION.md)
proves surjectivity for finite-dimensional complex representations at every value.
General generic-stabilizer occurrence and saturation
remain at the documented [feasibility stop](../../../../topics/all-degree-symmetry-protection/feasibility.md).

The persistent [audit](../../../../../Math/RepresentationTheory/Protection/Audit/AuditSymmetryProtection.lean)
checks layer ownership and 46 declarations transitively against only
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. No literature theorem is an
implicit axiom. No derivatives, EOM/IBP, UV generation or rates are covered.

and an explicit fermionic caveat. This dossier proves restriction-surjectivity
and scalar-charge statements, not the general friendship/generic-stabilizer

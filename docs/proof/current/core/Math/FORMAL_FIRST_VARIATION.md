# Formal Evolutionary First Variation

See the [proved result and exact hypotheses](../../../../topics/formal-first-variation/results.md)
and [verification](../../../../topics/formal-first-variation/verification.md).

`Math.JetFirstVariation` constructs arbitrary polynomial evolutionary
variations on four-direction jets, proves their commutation with every total
derivative and identifies the zeroth and first coefficients of actual epsilon
substitution. `Math.FormalConnection.curvature_variation` derives curvature
variation from Leibniz and derivative commutation. `Math.MatrixFirstVariation`
proves the normalized trace contraction and its explicit divergence/bulk
split for finite matrices over a commutative complex algebra.

All premises are explicit algebraic structures or commutation/Leibniz inputs.
No supplied variational identity or analytic integration premise is used.
The persistent `Math.Algebra.Variational.Audit.AuditFirstVariation` checks all 36 public
source declarations transitively and rejects Physics/GUT imports.

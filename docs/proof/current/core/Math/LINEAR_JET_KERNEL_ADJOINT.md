# Linear Jet Kernel And Exterior Adjoint

The actual odd/even quotient-generator algebra of the
[linear EOM package](../../../../topics/linear-eom-weyl/results.md) has an exact
presentation: its projection kernel equals the two-sided ideal generated by
the odd/even insertions of every polynomial descendant of the seed maps.
`kernel_eq_relationIdeal`, `descendantIdeal_eq` and `kernel_eq_descendantIdeal`
prove both generator presentations. The seed modules may be infinite and the
equations inhomogeneous; no group covariance is required for this identity.

The proof uses linear splittings of the generator quotient maps and free
exterior/symmetric reconstruction. Modulo the generated ideal, reconstruction
of the projection is the identity. This proves the reverse kernel inclusion;
annihilation of the generators proves the forward one. The previously proved
surjectivity is retained. IBP is a linear range, without ideal closure.

At one odd field and no even fields, every positive-order off-shell grade is
the actual divergence range. A positive multi-index has a predecessor obtained
by subtracting a single direction. Span and image transport give
`odd_ibpRelations_top` for arbitrary specified seed maps. The quotient is
subsingleton, with `odd_quotient_finrank = 0` and an empty basis. This statement
does not count invariants at larger field contents.

Coefficient conjugation on Finsupp jets fixes derivative labels, is semilinear
and involutive, commutes with shifts and conjugates polynomial multiplication.
For any semilinear generator involution commuting with specified even
derivatives, the existing exterior adjoint commutes with the induced
derivations. It reverses products, preserves the actual ungraded divergence
range, and descends to a semilinear involution on its vector-space quotient.
`naturality` also proves compatibility with any intertwining linear generator
map. This introduces no quotient algebra structure for IBP.

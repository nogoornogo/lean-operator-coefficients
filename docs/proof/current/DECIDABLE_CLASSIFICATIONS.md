# Decidable structural classifications

These exact classifications specialize the [general theorems](GENERAL_THEOREMS.md)
to a fixed input. Their finite conditions do not classify arbitrary residual
representations or arbitrary field dictionaries.

## Fixed SM support at every degree

The input has ten signed three-generation SM fermion species, one
residual-neutral Higgs doublet and its dual, independent Lorentz actions,
and scalar cyclic q/l actions, for every positive cyclic order k.
For each content and Higgs bidegree, nonzero derivative-free coefficients
are equivalent to residual/hypercharge neutrality and finite positive
occupation, component, color-coupling and SU2-path conditions.

See [structural support](applications/Physics/SM_STRUCTURAL_QL_SUPPORT.md)
and [finite Higgs reduction](applications/Physics/SM_FINITE_HIGGS_SUPPORT.md).
The general reduction retains arbitrary residual flavor parameters; the
completed decision criterion uses the stated scalar cyclic actions.
No Weyl input or numerical rank premise is needed for this structural route.

## Finite lepton-charge criteria

Normalize baryon number using B3=3B. At absolute B3=b, with b=3 or 6,

$$
\mathcal C_{|B_3|=b}\ne0
\iff\exists L\in S_b,\quad bq+Ll\equiv0\pmod{k},
$$

where $S_3=\{-17,-15,\ldots,17\}$ and
$S_6=\{-18,-16,\ldots,18\}$. Here $S_b$ denotes a charge set, not a group.
Every charge in these sets has an actual coefficient witness. Prohibition is
the universal non-neutrality complement, including both baryon signs.
See [finite charge support](applications/Physics/SM_FINITE_LEPTON_SUPPORT.md).

## Minimum engineering dimensions

For every positive k on the same dictionary, a derivative-free B1
coefficient exists below dimension ten exactly when

$$
3q+l\equiv0\quad\lor\quad3q-l\equiv0\quad\lor\quad3q+3l\equiv0\pmod{k}.
$$

For the 572 labeled inputs with $2\le k\le12$, $0\le q,l<k$ and
$3q+l\not\equiv0$, all 326 allowed B1 inputs have exact minima; 246 are
forbidden at all degrees. Each minimum has an attaining coefficient and a
lower bound for all selected contents, both baryon signs and Higgs bidegrees.
Lean uses twice the dimension, $3n+2(h+d)$.

These are minima within the derivative-free domain. They do not exclude
lower-dimensional operators with covariant derivatives and do not establish
EOM/IBP-independent EFT minima, matching, generated interactions or lifetimes.
General lower-bound and attainment interfaces accept arbitrary k, but their
uniform sharp matching is certified only for the stated finite domain.

See [degree bounds and exact minima](applications/Physics/SM_CHARGE_DEGREE_BOUNDS.md).
The earlier 160 exact rows and 166 intervals remain checkpoint evidence;
the subsequent weak-connection proof closed all 326 allowed rows.

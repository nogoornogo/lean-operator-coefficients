## Representative reusable results

For a finite complex symmetric matrix, Takagi factorization and the full
unitary stabilizer classification are proved:

$$
U^T M U=\mathrm{diag}(\sigma_i),\qquad U^\dagger U=I,\quad\sigma_i\ge0.
$$

The stabilizer has a unitary zero-mass block and real orthogonal blocks for
each distinct positive mass. See the [precise Takagi and stabilizer inputs](inputs/Math/ASSUMPTIONS.md#full-unitary-majorana-stabilizers-and-takagi-existence).
Specified VEV/flavor stabilizers are supplied-data constructions; they do not
dynamically select a vacuum or potential.

Earlier investigations also produced SU5/SU6 branching and charge-profile
certificates, self-dual chirality obstructions, compact Spin constructions,
and E6 candidate-group interfaces. Their detailed dossiers below retain local
assumptions, explicit literature premises and unresolved global/model bridges.
A result's place in this list does not claim a full UV-model realization or
make it a premise of the general coefficient theorem.

The [SU6 study](../../model-studies/su6-protection/README.md) records the stopped
protection search and its reusable tests. Exact chronology and outcomes belong
to the [topic guide](../../topics/_history/README.md), while this page indexes
mathematical content.

# BGW Z6 Selection on Actual SM/Higgs Coefficients

The [result map](../../../../topics/bgw-z6-selection/results.md) records the
proved signed-field dictionary and the actual scalar representations. For
arbitrary complex copy modules, nonzero coefficients require total 3B divisible
by nine. Consequently the composed absolute Delta B=1 or 2 coefficient space
is zero at every mixed degree, and its residual-Hom product is zero through
the existing coefficient-space decomposition.

This applies to the ten signed SM fermions and two Higgs scalar species,
with local SM-cover/Weyl invariance and constructed Z6 copy actions. It proves
a restriction of the selection argument in the
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0306003v1); it is not a certificate for
right-handed neutrinos, anomalies, UV origin, derivatives or rates.

For one complex copy of lepton and Higgs, the charged LLHH domain is proved
equivalent to the neutral-copy Weinberg domain: the two lepton and two Higgs
phases cancel. Its coefficient space is equivalent to complex numbers and the
inverse image of one supplies a nonzero invariant coefficient. The proof uses
the actual selected fields and fermionic/bosonic statistics; it does not infer
existence from charge neutrality alone. No VEV, mass generation or rate is
claimed. See [verification](../../../../topics/bgw-z6-selection/verification.md).

Choose a result category below. Detailed proof dossiers remain organized by
[role](README.md); investigations and verification chronology stay in

| Category | What to expect |

The general decomposition does not automatically decide arbitrary residual Hom
spaces. The completed structural classification uses a fixed SM dictionary and
scalar cyclic q/l actions. Minimum-dimension claims refer to derivative-free
coefficients, not EOM/IBP-independent EFT minima. Allowed coefficients do not
establish generated interactions, amplitudes or lifetimes. Model-input checks
retain unproved UV, potential and mass-selection premises explicitly.

[Assumptions](../ASSUMPTIONS.md) index hypotheses;
[literature coverage](../../verification/README.md) maps source locations to
bounded certificates. This navigation change creates no new proof baseline.

The [related-literature map](../RELATED_LITERATURE.md) links mathematical
background, related classifications and existing selected comparisons without
turning them into proof premises or a whole-paper verification claim.

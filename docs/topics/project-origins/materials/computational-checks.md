# Computational checks performed during the investigation

These checks are supporting evidence only. They do not replace symbolic proofs.

## 1. SU(6) branching machinery used

The exploration used exact symbolic branching based on:

- SU(6) → SU(5) interlacing / Gelfand-Tsetlin rules,
- SU(5) → SU(3) × SU(2) via Littlewood-Richardson coefficients,
- exact integer/rational charge arithmetic,
- SU(6) cubic anomaly checks using representation-theory formulas / trace ratios.

No floating-point slope matching was intended in the final checks.

---

## 2. Standard Z9 charge-resolved search

A search over complex SU(6) irreps was extended through progressively higher representation dimensions.

Reported checkpoints included approximately:

- irreps up to dimension 5,000,
- up to 20,000,
- up to 100,000.

The exotic-free + anomaly-free kernel grew with the representation set, but its projection to the tested SM × Z9 chiral spectra appeared to remain rank 1, matching the ordinary one-family pattern generated by

\[
15+2\bar6.
\]

This was numerical evidence for a later symbolic shift theorem.

---

## 3. Hypercharge-slope bounded search

For SU(6) Young diagrams with total box number up to 12:

- 196 irreps were enumerated,
- 560 local hypercharge slopes could supply all five SM field types somewhere in the branching,
- after imposing the full SM-only chiral-index condition plus SU(6)^3 anomaly cancellation, only the standard embedding reportedly survived.

For box number up to 20:

- 1,124 irreps were considered,
- 4,513 local candidate slopes appeared before full chiral filtering,
- representative flipped slopes were tested and failed.

The full 4,513-slope final filtering was not completed in the conversational pass.

---

## 4. Interpretation

The bounded searches strongly suggested that:

1. high representations do generate many shifted SM-like components locally;
2. those local components generally drag along unavoidable exotic chiral structure;
3. complete SM-only chiral spectra are much more constrained than local branching suggests.

This motivated the move to a representation-ring / Weyl-symmetry proof.

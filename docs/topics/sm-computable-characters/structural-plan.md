## Frozen endpoint and hypotheses

three-generation Q or L species at arbitrary exterior degree n and weak
constituent degree m. First work with the actual semisimple representations:

    Q: support ↔ n ≤ 36 ∧ 6 ∣ n ∧ 2 ∣ m ∧ m ≤ min(n,36−n);
    L: support ↔ n ≤ 12 ∧ 2 ∣ n ∧ 2 ∣ m ∧ m ≤ min(n,12−n).

The consumer must retain the actual independent spin factors, exterior
statistics, hypercharge equation and residual scalar q/l charge equation.

the existing extracted integer may use the unchanged Weyl input. A direct
actual-Hom support theorem should state exactly its own inputs.

Do not add a premise asserting the desired classification, assume injectivity
of arbitrary fermionic multiplication, or replace the actual source by a
tensor space without proving the adapter. No new literature premise is part
of this implementation pass. An unresolved literature-input alternative is
recorded as a proposal, not silently used.

## Proof stages

1. Build an extreme Cauchy map from a symmetric power into an exterior power
   of a tensor product, prove its action on pure powers, naturality and a
   nonzero/injective statement sufficient for the Q/L blocks. Use existing
   finite symmetrization, exterior maps and pinned TauCeti polarization.
   A top-volume-preserving factor is enough; full Schur functor theory is not
   required. Verify this adapter before proceeding to the next stages.
2. Transport independent generation summands and assemble lower-half support
   using existing exterior-product injectivity and SU(2) Clebsch–Gordan.
   Prove center/parity and sharp weak-weight bounds on the actual source.
3. Prove equivariant complementary-degree transport with its determinant
   character, assemble upper-half support, and connect the Q/L, conjugate,
   hypercharge, scalar q/l and Higgs consumers.

The scalar q/l actions remain those already defined. Arbitrary residual
actions, all mixed contents, numerical rank equalities and regenerated 572
assignment lists are outside this endpoint.

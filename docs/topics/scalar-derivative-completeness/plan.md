# Frozen Completeness Contract

## Common hypotheses and acceptance

Use complex ordinary, unbounded, commutative scalar jets. C1 has two real-field
labels, one direction, both free second-derivative equations and Unit symmetry.
C2 has one real-field label, four directions, the free (+---) wave equation
and the existing actual SL(2,C) direction action. Field reality is a separate
proved condition on complexified coefficients. Preserve full differential
EOM ideals and the linear divergence range, including their full preimage
on every selection. Never ideal-close IBP or assume invariant extension.

This user authorization reopens the specified completeness foundations.
Needed new helpers may cover finite first-jet reduction, homogeneous
source/relation comparison, the fixed finite Lorentz tensor contractions and
their coefficient duals. No arbitrary invariant solver, all-degree momentum
dictionary, interacting EOM grading, analytic action, new dependency or
physical amplitude theorem is included. No paper count is a premise.

## A — C1 complete positive-degree sectors

1. Reduce the free unbounded one-direction algebra to the four first-jet
   coordinates a,a',b,b' with derivation a' partial_a+b' partial_b. Prove
   derivative compatibility and annihilation of the entire differential EOM
   ideal, not just its seeds.
2. Construct global linear coefficient functionals annihilating all EOM and
   total derivatives. They must detect the chosen representatives for
   contents (1,1),d=1 and (2,2),d=1,2 with normalized value 1.
3. Classify the complete actual multihomogeneous source in each sector.
   Prove EOM/IBP reconstruction, an actual-image coordinate equivalence and
   independent coefficient values. The expected dimension is 1 in each.
   Reuse the proved d=0 and (1,1),d=2 results for the finite comparison.

## B — C2 derivative order two

1. Construct the complete actual four-field/two-derivative source and its
   EOM/IBP and group constraints. A finite list must have proved coverage.
2. Prove every admitted Lorentz-invariant coefficient vanishes on this whole
   source. Prefer low-rank metric contraction or a finite set of actual
   group transformations for the upper bound; infinitesimal rows alone are
   not sufficient evidence for full group identification.

## C — C2 derivative order four

1. Use the complete finite source and actual full-relation comparison, or
   prove complete reconstruction through the fixed metric contractions.
2. Construct a globally Lorentz-invariant EOM/IBP-annihilating coefficient
   detecting K squared, and prove the selected invariant coefficient space
   is exactly its scalar line, with both inverse/evaluation laws. Do not
   identify the entire un-invariant quotient with this one-dimensional line.
3. Reconcile Lorentz and paper Euclidean conventions explicitly for every
   claimed literature comparison. Native Lorentz statements may be proved
   first, with any remaining paper bridge qualified separately.

## Routes and stops

The structural route is explicit full-source reconstruction, metric
contractions, Leibniz identities and separating duals. The coordinate route
is an actual finite monomial basis with proved relation/group rows and exact
elimination certified within Lean. Finite actual group transformations can
give upper bounds without assuming they generate the whole group; positive
lower bounds still require a full-group coefficient or an explicit bridge.

Proceed through A, B, C. A milestone commit is not a stop. Each sector ends
with the stated certificate, a counterexample or a concrete unresolved
bounded foundation after both routes. Record exactly what was implemented
and which equation failed; an absent API alone is not new proof evidence.
Do not broaden scope or restart the route budget by renaming a gap.

Use two compiler threads and CPU affinity to two available cores, a 16 GiB
per-process address-space guard, 180-second focused and 540-second full build
ceilings, and default elaboration/kernel limits. Decompose heavy proofs;
do not raise caps. All new source namespaces, including helper instances,
receive persistent strict transitive axiom/import audits and aggregate
registration. Run focused checks, a serialized full build, retained exact
diagnostics, layout, navigation, metadata, link and whitespace checks.

Update the topic, dossiers, actual assumptions and literature coverage before
the final verified commit. Child integration may use the recorded parent
exception. Master integration, push and unrelated worktree cleanup are not
authorized by this plan.

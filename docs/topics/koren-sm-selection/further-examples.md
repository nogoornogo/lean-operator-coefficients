## Recommended: Symmetry Restriction and Flavor in a Leptoquark Model

Davighi, Greljo and Thomsen, *Leptoquarks with Exactly Stable Protons*,
[arXiv:2202.05275v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05275v2),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.05275v2), Secs. II–III, Tables I–II,
Eqs. (2)–(4), (6), (12)–(15).

The non-supersymmetric model has family-dependent U(1)X charges. Its residual
Z9 or Z18 action is family-universal on SM fermions. Sec. IIIa gives
Delta B=0 modulo three; Sec. IIIb explicitly distinguishes accidental muon
conservation from exact residual protection, displaying higher-order operators
that restore other lepton couplings. Do not infer all-degree LFV prohibition.

Proposed concrete instance: m=1,n=3, corresponding to a=b=0,r=1 in Eq. (12).
Table I then gives quark charge 1, electron/tau charge 0, muon charge -9,
Higgs charge 0 and singlet charges 0 and 9. Eq. (6) gives residual Z9.
These specializations are algebraic preflight calculations, not Lean results.

This tests something new: compare specified muon/electron and muon/tau
coefficient selectors under the UV action and its residual restriction. The
charge distinction disappears modulo nine, while B1/B2 protection remains.
Use explicit gauge-basis flavor vectors, without claiming a mass-basis theorem.
A neutral residual charge alone supplies no nonzero witness; use the existing
actual dipole equivalence for any existence claim. Dipole and mixed SM supports
remain separate coefficient domains, even when their symmetry is shared.

Start with the SM/Higgs restriction and selected dipole coefficients. Full
leptoquark, right-handed-neutrino and singlet field admission is a separate
extension. VEV stabilizer arithmetic may be certified independently; it does
not prove a vacuum, matching, neutrino masses or rates. The historical flavor
anomaly motivation is not a present experimental claim.

## Alternative: Lepton Quarticity and Compensating Scalar Charge

Centelles Chulia, Ma, Srivastava and Valle, *Dirac Neutrinos and Dark Matter
Stability from Lepton Quarticity*,
[arXiv:1606.04543v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.04543v1),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.04543v1), Sec. II, Table I and Eqs. (1), (10), (11).
This arXiv record has one version; it must not be cited as v2 or v3.

The model uses Z4 times Z2, with charged leptons and neutrinos of quarticity
one and a neutral Higgs. Its charged scalar eta has quarticity two. The
Majorana bilinear with an eta insertion is invariant, although a bare
Majorana bilinear is forbidden. Phi and chi VEVs preserve Z4 and break Z2.

A useful regression would distinguish a restricted SM/Higgs L=2 prohibition
from a dressed operator on an extended scalar alphabet. Fermionic lepton
number two is not the same target as total discrete charge two once eta is
included. Prove action and a coefficient witness before claiming a permitted
operator exists. The right-handed neutrino and eta fields need explicit new
adapters; the original prohibition must retain its field-domain restriction.
No baryon protection is established by this preflight. Dark-matter stability
as a physical particle statement and cosmological calculations remain outside
scope.

## Recommendation and Verification

Prefer the leptoquark paper's restricted UV/residual comparison: it exercises
family selection, restriction and the boundary between prohibition and nonzero
existence, rather than repeating only total B/L detection. Quarticity is a
second-stage test of field-domain dependence. Freeze either independent proof
endpoint and obtain explicit top-level branch direction before implementation.

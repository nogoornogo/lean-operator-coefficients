The support-guarded all-degree iff from the previous topic still applies.

## Two assessed routes and their missing certificates

1. **Explicit weak symmetric-power decomposition.** A uniform decomposition
   of Sym^h(H) tensor Sym^a(H-dual), with actual weak action, hypercharge and
   finite fermion constituents, would reduce the bosonic support problem.
   The selected route still needs intertwining equivalences and complete
   constituent/multiplicity formulas, not just weight counts. The inspected
   local FourWeakWeylCompleteness gives four-doublet coordinates, while
   SymmetricPowerLowDegree gives degrees zero and one. Their source statements
   do not provide this uniform all-degree decomposition. No all-degree
   Clebsch-Gordan adapter for this input was found in the searched local
   representation sources and inspected starting points.
2. **Finite covariant-module generation.** This would require a certified
   scalar invariant algebra and, for each relevant fermion content, an actual
   finite seed module whose dressing images span every admitted coefficient.
   SymmetricPowerDescent and the new InvariantQuadraticPowers certify descent
   and construction, not spanning or generation. The new normalized Higgs
   powers do not prove that the scalar invariant algebra consists only of
   those powers, or that mixed covariants are finitely generated over them.

The next proof obligation must connect either reduction to the exact local
and statistics carrier, retain relations among seeds, and certify zero/nonzero
support. Color and Lorentz constituents of the finite fermion carriers also
need actual support certificates. A Hilbert series or finite low-degree list
cannot replace these interfaces. Library search is evidence about the inspected
assets, not a claim that such mathematical theorems do not exist elsewhere.

## Stop decision

Stages 1, 2, 3 and 4A have proved endpoints. Stage 4B reaches the plan's
source-backed feasibility endpoint: the actual covariant spanning/reduction
interface above is missing. The plan explicitly permits a missing-theorem feasibility endpoint when no
finite reduction has been certified. This endpoint is reached here. A direct
local weak-group decomposition remains a possible continuation; this assessment
does not prove that a general reductive invariant-theory library is required.
Such a general foundational library was not started implicitly here.
Stage 4C depends on that certificate and remains unstarted.

This is not an impossibility result. The fixed-input complete support theorem
and all-degree q/l classification remain open. The 572 labeled assignments and
151 generator orbits are not reclassified or recounted. Current positive
lepton-parity and neutral dimension-six allowance controls remain intact.
A possible next contract is to prove the actual two-doublet symmetric-power
weak/hypercharge decomposition first, then connect its finite covariants to
one fermion content before committing to every content.

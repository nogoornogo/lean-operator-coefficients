# Four Extensions: Contracts, Dependencies and Gates

## Meaning of completion

Preserve the three main repository theorems. This work makes their inputs and
outputs usable in specific representation families; it does not claim new
protecting UV models. The user authorized investigating the previously deferred
support-classification direction. That authorization does not justify assuming
finite generation, nonzero dressing or a degree cutoff.

The final support objective is an explicit necessary-and-sufficient predicate
for nonzero actual local/statistics carriers at every signed fermion and Higgs
content in the fixed input. Project it to all-degree absolute B1 prohibition
for universal cyclic q/l charges. A finite list of low-degree operators, a
conditional support oracle, a Hilbert-series count without a semantic bridge,
or the previous guarded iff is not this completion endpoint. An explicit full
basis of every coefficient space is a further objective, not required here.

Before implementing each stage, freeze its Lean statement, carrier conventions,
actual representation dictionaries and required bridges. Record feasibility
obstacles and distinguish a proved result from an unproved candidate reduction.

## Stage 1: explicit finite nonabelian LFV (first bounded milestone)

Choose S3 = permutations of Fin 3, acting on C^3 by permutation of coordinates.
This gives genuine finite noncommuting flavor actions and a small auditable
matrix problem. Use the existing actual B/W dipole coefficient selector with
arbitrary source vector mu and target functional ell.

First prove the representation and its generator relations. Determine its
intertwining endomorphisms: the proposed exact space is spanned by the identity
and the all-ones matrix. Prove both inclusion directions using actual generator
commutation; a numerical centralizer dimension is not a premise. Then derive
selected allowance iff at least one of

    ell(mu), (sum_i mu_i) * ell(ones)

is nonzero. Retain empty/zero-selector controls and an off-diagonal coordinate
allowance witness. These are proposed statements to verify, not current proofs.

Endpoint: exact selected coefficient iff for this concrete S3 action, audited
through the actual dipole interface. This is a constructed flavor input, not
an anomaly-free gauged model. If the centralizer statement fails, record a
counterexample and correct the frozen statement before proceeding.

## Stage 2: semisimple decomposition as an LFV input

Reuse the existing finite semisimple decomposition, Schur and actual
intertwiner-evaluation assets. First interface: source and target supplied as
finite sums of copies of common irreducible representatives over C, with
certified representation equivalences, finite-dimensional blocks and proved
nonisomorphism between distinct representatives. Multiplicities, selectors and
block actions are data; existence of an abstract decomposition does not provide
a computable one automatically.

Target: explicit equivalence between the actual intertwiner space and the
finite product of allowed multiplicity matrices. On every common irreducible
block, compute the selector evaluation of each matrix-unit intertwiner.
Selected allowance iff at least one of these evaluations is nonzero; prohibition
iff all vanish. This retains the internal irreducible-vector pairing as well
as multiplicity support. Shared irreducibles alone are not a selected iff.

Include the existing diagonal cyclic result as a one-dimensional-block control.
Connect Stage 1 when its trivial/standard decomposition is actually certified;
do not assume standard-block irreducibility or Schur endomorphism scalarity.

Endpoint: the general adapter for certified decomposition data and at least
one concrete consumer. Extend from this explicit interface to arbitrary
finite-dimensional semisimple modules only after the transport is proved.
No arbitrary nonsemisimple or weight-only classification is requested.

## Stage 3: an infinite operator family with nonzero coefficients

Freeze the actual three-generation signed SM/Weyl alphabet, one Higgs doublet
and its signed conjugate, neutral Higgs residual action and the existing actual
cyclic universal fermion charges. Signed conjugates are algebraic fields in the
existing complex coefficient dictionary, not numerical complex conjugation
constraints imposed afterwards.

Select the proved actual DUQL dimension-six coordinate used in the existing
neutral-charge converse as the seed. Construct the neutral scalar invariant
H-dagger H and the family

    DUQL * (H-dagger H)^r, for every natural r.

Prove the scalar contraction, the mixed coefficient product/descent and
nonvanishing for every r on the actual exterior/symmetric carrier. A product
of symbolic expressions does not establish nonzero coefficients after
statistics. Supply a nonzero evaluation or an injective multiplication bridge.
Track the degree, B/L grades and total residual charge. Target: actual allowance
iff 3q+l=0 for each member at its fixed content; neutrality must have the
constructed witness, and nonneutrality uses the existing scalar criterion.

Endpoint: an infinite certified nonzero family, not an exhaustive all-degree
B1 classification. A successful dressing construction is an input to Stage 4;
it does not prove that every operator is such a dressing.

## Stage 4: complete fixed-input support classification

### 4A: finite fermion content

Use exactly three copies of every signed species in the existing full SM/Weyl
field sum. Prove its dimension and the vanishing of exterior powers above that
dimension. Derive finite bounds on species counts and even-fermion parity in
the actual coefficient dictionary. Do not hard-code an estimated dimension
or confuse exterior powers of the whole field sum with raw ordered tensors.
The existing dimension-seven/eleven windows are bounded gates, not this result.

Endpoint: a certified finite fermion-content universe before selecting any
bound on Higgs count. Record the number/structure of contents without promising
that brute-force enumeration is feasible.

### 4B: unbounded Higgs content and finite reduction

The neutral Higgs keeps residual charge unchanged but its symmetric powers
change local gauge support. Investigate the concrete two-doublet scalar algebra
and its covariants with the finite fermion carriers. First certify the actual
scalar invariant algebra or a sufficient explicit generating description.
Then prove, rather than assume, that each relevant mixed invariant module has
finitely many seed contents over that scalar invariant algebra.

Two candidate proof routes are allowed for assessment: explicit weak-group
symmetric-power/tensor decomposition with hypercharge selection; or a concrete
finite-generation module certificate. Select a route only after inspecting
existing interfaces. A Hilbert-series or computer-algebra calculation may guide
the candidate generators, but must not become an unchecked premise.

Necessary proof obligations include actual scalar/gauge/Lorentz actions,
statistics quotient, a complete spanning/transport certificate and nonzero
multiplication when used to claim support propagation. Stage 3 supplies only
one such family. No full reductive invariant-theory library is automatically
included; a need for that foundation triggers a user scope decision.

Endpoint: a proved finite reduction for all relevant Higgs counts, or a precise
obstruction and its missing theorem. If no reduction is certified, stop this
stage as feasibility-limited; do not call low-degree checks complete.

### 4C: all-content support and cyclic protection

For the finite seeds/content cases from 4A/4B, prove zero or nontrivial actual
carriers, retaining color, weak, hypercharge, Lorentz, Young/statistics and
finite-generation constraints. Produce an explicit support iff for arbitrary
contents in the fixed alphabet. This classifies support, not coefficient bases.

Combine the support theorem with AdmittedCyclicSMProhibition to derive exact
all-degree B1 prohibition conditions for every positive cyclic order and
universal q/l charges with neutral Higgs. Only after this theorem is proved,
revisit the order-2-through-12 table and its 151 fixed-order generator orbits.
Keep original labeled counts unchanged until a new predicate is certified.
Use the known all-degree lepton-parity certificate as a positive control and
the actual dimension-six neutral DUQL coordinate as an allowance control.

Endpoint: complete fixed-input support iff and exact all-degree B1 parameter
criterion, or the explicitly recorded 4B/4C proof boundary. Passing arithmetic
or support tests does not establish quantum or UV realization.

## Order, scope decisions and verification

Run 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4. The two LFV stages have bounded goals independent of
full SM support. Stage 3 obtains an all-degree construction before a completeness
attempt. Stage 4 is a substantial project; its reduction and support certificates
are separate milestones, not implicit assumptions.

If a stage crosses a foundational or physical-scope gate, record its missing
statement and continue independent authorized stages before returning for user
judgment. Exhausted coverage, a counterexample or a bounded feasibility result
may be an honest stop; none is to be labeled complete classification.

Place sources by Math/Physics/GUT ownership, register aggregates and strict
transitive audits, run focused checks before one serialized full build at each
coherent proof milestone, then layout/links/diff checks. Retain unchanged pins
and axiom policy; no sorry, custom axioms, audit exceptions or unchecked oracle
inputs. Keep English contracts, source records, results and verification in
this topic. Commit verified milestones on the approved branch, without a master
merge or remote push. No reliable wall-clock estimate exists for Stage 4 before
the finite-reduction route is certified.

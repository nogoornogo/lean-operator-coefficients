# Nonzero DUQL Higgs Dressing at Every Power

Fix the existing actual three-generation cyclic universal flavor actions and
one neutral Higgs doublet with its algebraic dual. At every natural r the
actual DUQL-dressing family has

    allowance iff 3q+l = 0 mod k,
    prohibition iff 3q+l != 0 mod k.

This is a constructed family at fermion degree 4 and scalar degree 2r, not a
classification of every operator at that degree or every all-degree B1 content.
The usual canonical dimension is 6+2r. No derivatives or EOM/IBP are included.

## Actual source and statistics connection

The seed is reconstructed from the proved flavored coefficient dictionary
with only its DUQL factor populated; seed_dictionary proves that the other
three factors vanish. The reconstruction is injective. Neutrality admits an
actual flavor coordinate; nonneutrality makes this seed index set empty.
This is not a raw ordered tensor assumed to survive statistics.

NeutralHiggsPowers identifies the actual Higgs/dual field sum, including its
one-dimensional neutral multiplicities, with the Higgs representation plus
its dual. It pulls back the symmetric invariant evaluation pairing. The
pairing has a proved nonzero diagonal and yields symmetric-power functionals
at every r. InvariantQuadraticPowers symmetrizes an r-fold bilinear product and
descends through the actual symmetric quotient. A pure-tensor evaluation is
nonzero; no assertion that an invariant product automatically survives the
quotient is used.

The raw symmetrized diagonal has a factorial normalization and a factor 2^r.
Both are proved nonzero over C and removed. The normalized diagonal polynomial
is exactly (phi(h))^r, namely (H-dagger H)^r in the existing algebraic field
dictionary. Signed conjugates remain independent algebraic fields, not an
extra numerical Hermitian constraint.

MixedInvariantDressing forms the actual tensor-product coefficient and proves
nonzero products from nonzero evaluations. DressedDuqlFamily defines the
linear range of these constructed mixed coefficients and proves its allowance
and prohibition iff. It also proves unbounded_allowance: for neutral seed
charge, actual coefficients occur above every proposed scalar-degree bound.
The scalar is gauge/Lorentz/residual neutral, and the DUQL seed retains its
existing signed family grades; family_grades proves that the certified DUQL
ordering retains (3B,L)=(3,1) for every scalar dressing. This construction does
not change its residual charge.

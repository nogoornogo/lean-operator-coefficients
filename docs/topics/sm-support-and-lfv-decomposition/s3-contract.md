# Frozen S3 Contract

Use all coordinate permutations of Fin 3 acting on complex triples by inverse
reindexing. Prove the action and noncommutativity of the two adjacent swaps.
Prove the intertwiner action is (d-o) times identity plus o times the constant
coordinate sum, by deriving its diagonal/off-diagonal entries from permutation
commutation. Derive the actual B/W selected allowance iff ell(mu) or
(sum mu)*ell(ones) is nonzero. Zero selectors and off-diagonal allowance are
controls. No irreducible decomposition or physical UV realization is assumed.

    (36,18,18,12,6,36,18,18,12,6), total 180.

The signs include the conjugate gauge fields and opposite chirality as the
existing field dictionary prescribes. This is the complex dimension of the
full coefficient-input field sum, not the count of independent UV fermions.

Every exterior element above degree 180 is zero. Hence every actual mixed
coefficient at fermion degree n>180 is zero for arbitrary scalar degree and
arbitrary residual actions on the fixed three-copy multiplicities. A nonzero
coefficient implies n<=180. Each ordered species count is bounded by n, giving
a certified finite count universe Species -> Fin 181.

This is a deliberately loose finite superset. Its tuples are not all admitted
contents and no exhaustive enumeration was performed. Existing odd-fermion
Lorentz parity remains an independent exclusion; the exterior bound does not
replace color, weak, hypercharge or permutation support constraints.

no Higgs cutoff. Indeed the [dressing family](dressing-results.md) proves actual

# Frozen Finite Irreducible Block Contract

Use finite source/target products of finite-dimensional complex irreducible
representations and supplied representation equivalences to actual flavor
spaces. First prove that all actual intertwiners are equivalent to their
rectangular family of block intertwiners. For each matching pair choose a
proved equivalence, use Schur to identify its Hom with one complex scalar,
and prove unmatched blocks zero. Matching matrices reconstruct all maps.
Selected allowance iff a matching block matrix unit has nonzero selector
evaluation. No basis of intertwiners or spanning certificate is supplied as
an unchecked premise. Preserve the scalar internal pairing in the evaluation.

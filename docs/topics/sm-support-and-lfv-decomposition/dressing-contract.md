# Frozen Quadratic Dressing Contract

Construct invariant symmetric-power functionals of degree 2r from an actual
invariant bilinear scalar pairing, by permuting/symmetrizing the r-fold paired
multilinear map and descending through the symmetric-power quotient. Prove a
nonzero pure-tensor value at a vector where the pairing diagonal is nonzero.
Use the actual neutral Higgs/dual evaluation pairing and original DUQL exterior
seed to obtain mixed coefficients. Prove source identification, invariance,
statistics descent, nonzero evaluation and grade/charge transport. An abstract
pairing or conditional seed does not by itself complete the physical family.

# Research Trajectory

The project grew from specific GUT questions into reusable algebraic
classifications. This is a question-and-result trajectory, not a calendar or
active task queue. Current theorems and the [assumptions ledgers](../../proof/ASSUMPTIONS.md)
take precedence over early sketches. Stage numbers give reading order only;
no proof build is reproduced by this narrative.

## Stage 01: SU(6) Selection Rules

The starting question concerned symmetry protection of proton stability in
SU(6). Embedding/centralizer constraints, hypercharge characters, standard-branch
charge relations and a separate flipped relative-Weyl obstruction were proved.
The endpoint is a scoped selection-rule theorem, not an unconditional decay
prediction. See the [original sketch](../project-origins/materials/THEORY.md),
[initial foundations](../su6-foundations/chronology/initial-foundations.md),
[analytic characters](../su6-foundations/chronology/analytic-characters.md) and
[current scoped assembly](../../proof/current/applications/GUT/SCOPED_NO_GO_THEOREM.md).

## Stage 02: Mass Radicals And Reusable Foundations

Work distinguished virtual chiral indices from actual massless fields. It
developed symmetric bilinear masses, radical support, source adapters, specified
scalar/VEV constructions and charge profiles. Stronger unsupported spectrum
interpretations were corrected. Character restriction, product-factor
representations and multiplicity centralizers became reusable mathematics.
See [mass review](../mass-profile-foundations/chronology/nonzero-mass-review.md),
[tensor benchmarks](../su6-foundations/chronology/mass-and-tensor-benchmarks.md),
[generalizations](../character-centralizer-foundations/chronology/character-and-centralizer-generalizations.md)
and [scalar families](../mass-profile-foundations/chronology/scalar-families-and-profiles.md).

## Stage 03: Other Groups And Trust Boundaries

The standard SU(5) chain was transported to SU(N), N ≥ 5. A concrete Spin(10)
inclusion and half-spin source were constructed; symplectic groups supplied a
separate chirality obstruction. These are specified routes, not all embeddings.
See [standard chains](../standard-chain-foundations/chronology/su6-and-sun-adapters.md),
[Spin source](../spin-standard-chain/chronology/branching-and-positive-target.md) and
[symplectic chirality](../symplectic-chirality/chronology/torus-and-chirality.md).

E6 work built finite Lie certificates, a compact candidate, its SU(5) inclusion,
27-dimensional source, center, torus and contracting loops. Global simple
connectedness remains conditional on a literature input; named-E6 identification
is not an unconditional Lean equivalence. See
[construction history](../e6-global-topology/chronology/compact-geometry.md) and the
[literature boundary](../../proof/current/inputs/GUT/E6_LITERATURE_BOUNDARY.md).

The actual group-independent counterexample (`checkpoint 01`) and
structural conjugation route (`checkpoint 02`) entered master, together with the
Math/Physics/GUT layout (`checkpoint 03`). The counterexample prevents inferring the
no-go result from mass/spectrum conditions alone. See
[counterexample](../group-independent-assumptions/spectrum-bridge.md)
and [structural inputs](../../proof/current/inputs/Physics/assumptions/conjugation.md#structural-inputs-for-conjugation-routes).

## Stage 04: From Existence To Qque Completeness

Two conjugations and four monitored qque weights replaced stronger source
conditions. Finite/compact residual groups, full SM tensors, spinors and
fermionic exterior descent were connected to actual light fields. Exact spectra,
signed indices and shared/pairwise positive constituents gave alternative
sufficient conditions; balanced/vectorlike extensions and the Georgi–Glashow
benchmark supplied applications. See
[light-field connection](../full-sm-light-bridge/implementation.md),
[balanced extension](../../proof/current/applications/Physics/BALANCED_SPECIES_EXTENSION.md) and
[vectorlike spectrum](../../proof/current/inputs/Physics/VECTORLIKE_SPECTRUM.md).

The next step brought intrinsic qque completeness (`checkpoint 04`), full light-species
classification (`checkpoint 05`) and the common-irreducible criterion (`checkpoint 06`).
The output became an explicit linear equivalence and necessary/sufficient
criteria, rather than only a sufficient nonzero witness. See
[qque classification](../qque-coefficient-completeness/results.md).

## Stage 05: Matching, Protection And Yukawa Families

Algebraic generation was treated separately from allowed-coefficient existence.
The SU(5) tree pilot (`checkpoint 07`) and general mediator API (`checkpoint 08`) extended
through inheritance, SU(6)/SU(5+r), conjugated inputs and full light-species
reconstruction. Cancellation and radical-inheritance counterexamples fixed
their limits. See [matching](../mediator-qque-matching/README.md),
[inheritance](../mediator-inheritance/README.md) and
[light-field boundary](../light-inheritance-boundary/README.md).

Invariant mass protection (`checkpoint 09`) led to UV-generated Yukawa protection,
linear obstruction fibers and exact-radical criteria. Specified SU(6) Yukawa
families developed into a proved five-dimensional fixed-vacuum mass image and
full radical decomposition. Full coupling-space dimension 22 remains unproved;
the external coordinate calculation does not establish it. See
[mass protection](../invariant-mass-protection/README.md),
[vacuum protection](../yukawa-vacuum-protection/README.md) and
[SU(6) spectra](../../proof/current/inputs/GUT/assumptions/concrete-model-inputs.md#specified-su6-yukawa-spectra).

## Stage 06: Four Bounded Classifications

- (`checkpoint 10`): CLFV B/W dipoles, with explicit matching/domain duties.
- (`checkpoint 11`, `checkpoint 12`): the specified six-right-quark ΔB = 2 sector,
  including typed exchanges and actual-field transport. Its trivial scalar
  one-generation case has two coordinates, not every flavored sector.
- (`checkpoint 13`): Weinberg LLHH classification and its Majorana connection
  at a supplied nonzero VEV.
- (`checkpoint 20`): the dimension-six derivative-free ΔL = 1 sector on all
  ten signed SM species, decomposed into four residual-Hom families.

See [sector assumptions](../../proof/current/inputs/Physics/assumptions/bounded-sectors.md#additional-bounded-operator-classifications).

## Stage 07: Common Completeness And Stabilizers

The shared permutation/statistics and equivariant-transport layer (`checkpoint 14`)
connected all four classifications with proved extraction/reconstruction and
inverse agreement. Distinct input realizations and sector-specific gauge/Lorentz
proofs remain. The full Li operator-basis, derivative and EOM/IBP program was
not undertaken. See [integration](../operator-completeness-interface/integration.md).

Specified VEV/flavor stabilizers (`checkpoint 15`), diagonal Majorana unitary
stabilizers (`checkpoint 16`) and finite symmetric-matrix Takagi existence (`checkpoint 17`)
followed. They give exact group/Hom transport, not dynamical vacuum selection
or simultaneous Yukawa classification. See
[VEV group](../vev-stabilizer/global-group.md),
[flavor symmetry](../flavor-stabilizer/results.md) and
[Takagi existence](../majorana-congruence-interface/existence.md).

These checkpoint outcomes explain the progression of mathematical questions.
They do not replace the individual assumptions and verification records or
create a new completion log.

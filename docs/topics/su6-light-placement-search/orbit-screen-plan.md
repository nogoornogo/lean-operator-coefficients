# Z5 Generator Orbits and SU(6) Screen

Freeze equivalence as multiplication of every species charge by a nonzero
Z5 element, corresponding to changing the chosen cyclic generator. Do not
silently identify gauge-dressed actions or different UV matter spectra.
Normalize nonzero qque charge to one; enumerate representatives and prove
scalar pattern tests are invariant under this rescaling.

Then check each representative against the prior standard-placement
scalar/block SU(6) phase formulas and determinant constraint. A failure
rejects this specified realization only. No alternate carrier, non-Abelian
UV action, anomaly cancellation or physical operator admission is invented.
Stop at audited algebraic lemmas, exact orbit/phase census, full build and
updated model/verification records on the existing branch. No merge or push.

The charge screen also checks hypercharge dressing as a separate labelled
operation. Its selected-charge invariance is proved; no complete UV or
physical quotient equivalence is inferred from the charge census.

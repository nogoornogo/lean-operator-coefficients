## Full-Light Completeness

The current exterior coefficients are on participating species ranges. To
state a whole-light-carrier result, derive an actual compatible light SM action
and its multiplicity dictionary, then prove the equivariant splitting/exterior
pullback needed for a full light coefficient. The existing
[full-radical criterion](../../../Physics/StandardModel/Operators/QqueCommonIrreducible.lean)
requires its actual compatibility, continuity and semisimplicity interfaces;
those premises are not supplied merely by citing the theorem.

After those bridges, nonzero qque is equivalent to nonzero
`Hom_H(Sym² Q_light, up-left_light tensor electron-left_light)`, and under the
stated semisimplicity this is the common-constituent criterion. The new common
kernel splits give a sufficient mechanism for nonvanishing. They do not claim
to be necessary conditions for all models. Independent species actions require
computing this post-product Hom, not checking pairwise species overlap.

## UV And Symmetry Boundaries

The current SM-cover actions need an explicit global-group descent whenever
the model demands the quotient rather than its cover. A specific UV residual
symmetry mechanism must derive its actual common-copy or compensated action
and the vacuum/scalar compatibility. The non-scalar external-product model
cannot be renamed a general compensated-copy theorem. Enlarged SU(N), Spin(10)
and E6 embeddings alone do not establish these premises; existing literature
boundaries remain unchanged.

## What Would Count As Protection

The proved endpoints establish allowed nonzero coefficient spaces. They do
not show that UV matching generates a nonzero Wilson coefficient. Conversely,
vanishing of the qque space alone does not establish all-BNV prohibition or
proton stability. A model protection claim needs all relevant operator sectors
and the model's actual light-field/action dictionaries. Higher-degree or other
sectors use their own completeness interfaces. Matching, rates and lifetimes
remain outside this exact algebraic endpoint.

These are recorded follow-ups for consultation after this endpoint, not
silently authorized new foundational or phenomenological projects.

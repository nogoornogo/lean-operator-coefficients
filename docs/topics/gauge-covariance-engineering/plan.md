# Frozen Gauge Covariance And Engineering Contract

## 1. Algebraic Local Gauge Covariance

In the existing associative ordinary differential algebra, take an actual
unit `u`, with inverse `v`, without assuming their derivatives vanish. Define
`A'_i = u*A_i*v - (partial_i u)*v`. Derive the inverse derivative identity,
covariant derivative transformation on adjoint elements, curvature conjugation,
and source-free signed Yang–Mills EOM conjugation. Prove identity and composition
of connection transformations and a nonconstant formal matrix-unit example.
These are identities inside a differential algebra. They do not assert a
polynomial automorphism of the fixed connection-only jet ring or descent of
an unconstructed local gauge group action through its EOM/IBP quotient.

## 2. Trace IBP

For finite matrices over an ordinary commutative complex differential algebra,
prove the trace of a commutator is zero, the trace of a covariant derivative
equals the ordinary derivative of its trace, and
`trace((nabla_i X)*Y) + trace(X*(nabla_i Y)) = partial_i trace(X*Y)`.
Connect this identity to the actual polynomial divergence range and nonlinear
EOM/IBP quotient, with inverse coefficient transport inherited from the parent.
Prove trace conjugation invariance for units. Instantiate the formulas on
the existing formal matrix Yang–Mills jets; do not ideal-close IBP.

## 3. Engineering Degree And Complete Bounded Consumer

Assign every scalar/connection component dimension one and each ordinary
derivative dimension one. Thus a jet variable has weight `1 + jet order`.
Prove that ordinary derivatives raise this weighted degree by one, products
add degrees, and each fixed degree has finitely many monomials for finite
species/directions. Construct finite-dimensional EOM and EOM/IBP image sources.
Record nonlinear scalar seed degree three, connection degree one, curvature
degree two and Yang–Mills equation degree three. No global quotient direct-sum
decomposition is required or inferred merely from finite images.

Give one complete neutral four-direction scalar consumer at engineering degree
two for the existing equation `box phi + lambda*phi^3`, for arbitrary complex
lambda. Prove its entire off-shell source is spanned by `phi^2` and the four
first jets, the first jets vanish under actual IBP, and the image of `phi^2`
in the full EOM/IBP quotient is nonzero. Produce reconstruction, a basis and
dimension one for this actual image source. Relations are taken from the full
unbounded EOM/IBP source, so higher-degree relations cannot be silently ignored.
This is not a general Yang–Mills invariant count or a degree-two claim about
all Lorentz/gauge representations.

## Routes, Limits And Stop

Primary routes: inverse/unit derivative identities and associative expansion;
trace cyclicity and entrywise derivatives; positive weighted monomial bounds
and an exact coefficient functional annihilating the full scalar relations.
Fallbacks: direct finite matrix expansion for the same covariance/trace
identities; joint field/order components for the same engineering source.
At most these two routes per foundation. A new independent theorem-level
foundation beyond these interfaces is a documented bounded feasibility stop.
Never replace a required covariance, nonzero or completeness statement by
an assumption. Continue through all three stages and verification; checkpoints
are not stops.

Keep dependency pins and default heartbeats/recursion/kernel limits unchanged.
Serialize heavy checks with four Lean threads, 540 seconds per command and an
inherited 16 GiB per-process address-space guard. Require persistent strict
transitive standard-axiom/import audits, focused builds, full `lake build`,
aggregate/layout, local links, topic index, metadata and diff checks. Reconcile
bibliography, dossiers and assumptions; review and commit. Leave the verified
child unmerged for review. Parent/master integration and remote updates are
not part of the endpoint.

Excluded: all Lagrangian/first-variation derivations, analytic fields/actions,
general local gauge orbit quotients, SU(N) real-form/traceless restrictions,
matrix-model Lorentz dictionaries, mixed nonlinear fermion EOM, general
normal forms/Hilbert series, amplitudes, matching, RGE and numerical dynamics.

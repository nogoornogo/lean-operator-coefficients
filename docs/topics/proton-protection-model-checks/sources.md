# Pinned Sources

These papers specify candidate model data. Registering a source does not certify
its physical claims or promote a literature statement to a proved premise.

1. Michael Duerr, Pavel Fileviez Pérez and Mark B. Wise,
   *Gauge Theory for Baryon and Lepton Numbers with Leptoquarks* (2013):
   [arXiv:1304.0576v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.0576v2),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.231801).
   Sections II–IV, especially Table I and the Section IV N=1 solution, define
   the initial charge/VEV model.
2. Bartosz Fornal and Benjamin Grinstein,
   *SU(5) Unification without Proton Decay* (2017):
   [arXiv:1706.08535v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08535v2),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.119.241801).
   Sections II–IV supply fields and mass/light-mixing conditions; Section V
   supplies the parity discussion and nonlinear-breaking qualification.
   Their [2018 exposition, arXiv:1808.00953v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.00953v1)
   is supplementary reading, not independent evidence.
3. Tomasz P. Dutka and John Gargalionis,
   *Dimension-5 baryon-number violation in low-scale Pati-Salam* (2023):
   [arXiv:2211.02054v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.02054v2),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.107.035019).
   Pin the chosen variant's field, scalar and operator equation/page locations
   at its phase gate; the present plan does not select every variant.

Record any later source-version change explicitly before revising a comparison.

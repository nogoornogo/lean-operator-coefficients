# Frozen Phase Contracts

## B/L

Use the product covering group with integral charges b=6B and l=2L; this includes
N=1 exotic charges. VEV charges are (-18,-6) and (0,-4), both nonzero VEVs,
and electroweak Higgs charges (0,0). The pure baryonic order-18 subgroup fixes
both VEVs. Physical charges on (Q,uR,dR,L,eR) restrict to (2,2,2,0,0) modulo18.
The formal target is the existing one-generation four-family coefficient
sector's vanishing on this subgroup. Global quotient descent and a full exotic
mass spectrum are not premises of this selection test. Check the full scalar
stabilizer independently by exact finite enumeration. Compare the Q/uc full
species actions with the common-copy embedding requirement.

## Split SU(5)

Accept Eq.(1) as a supplied SM decomposition, with one chiral generation and two
vector-like 40/50 copies. Work before electroweak breaking. Treat each rectangular
mass block as [a|C] with two rows and three columns, and freeze exact rank-two
blocks when identifying the unique light line. Check the determinant condition
and a fully specified exact rational control, not a proved UV parameter point.
Test Eq.(22)'s parity both on light fields and on the three exceptional terms.
The formal target is only the conditional light parity selection certificate;
it is not a claimed action on the original invariant full mass. Stop before new
40/50 representation foundations or nonlinear quantum matching.

## Pati–Salam

Fix the minimal setup of 2211.02054v2, Sec.II Eqs.(1),(2),(5),(10): fL=(4,2,1),
fR=(4,1,2), chi=(4,1,2) with neutral VEV, Phi=(1,2,2), and gauge singlet xi.
Use one generation and the unbroken-SM stage (Phi VEV zero); charged SM species
are massless, while the nuR/xi symmetric mass is allowed. No additional fermions
or scalar variants are considered. Treat this as a supplied broken-phase SM
dictionary, not a Lean-proved PS branching/stabilizer. Compare H=1 (the specified
remaining gauge action is SM) and the optional accidental baryon action; the
latter is not mandatory for all gauge-invariant higher-dimensional interactions.
The formal target is a nonzero physical qque coefficient for H=1 on identical
one-copy light representations. Independently check the nonzero epsilon
contraction of Eq.(19) as an exact finite polynomial control. Stop before PS
representation foundations, matching or decay rates.

The ordered three-model pass is closed at its bounded stops. The results do not
support a universal rule that stable-proton models must fail the common-copy
inlet. Which symmetry and mass-stage dictionary is tested matters.

## Closed Versus Open

Closed: exact specified residual/charge controls, original-mass parity checks,
rectangular-kernel controls, a selected PS polynomial nonzero control, and the
bounded common-copy feasibility/obstruction decisions. Conditional Lean results
use existing physical sector interfaces; exact scripts are supplementary.

Open: UV branching/stabilizer and global quotient constructions, full original
40/50/PS actual-field adapters, simultaneous split-model UV parameter witnesses,
post-EW exact mixing and all-loop/nonlinear matching. These are explicitly
beyond the two-route feasibility pass and are not silently converted into
premises of a claimed whole-model theorem. No rates or numerical phenomenology
are attempted. Completing these would require a separately bounded follow-up.

The general completeness theorem describes allowed coefficient spaces. It does
not imply that every allowed coefficient is generated by a chosen Lagrangian.
This distinction accounts for the gauge-only SU(5)/PS comparisons without
changing the general theorem or declaring a false model contradiction.

actual dipole coefficient selector. Do not extend it to arbitrary nonabelian

## Stage 3: existence of an SM radical from copy constraints

Fix the existing actual full-SM restriction of the 10/bar5/40/50 representations
and their algebraic duals, arbitrary finite copy counts p,q and generation
number N. There is no additional residual generation action. The relevant mass
is full-SM-invariant and symmetric complex bilinear, as in ActualFullFamilyIndex.

Candidate exact existence statement:

    exists such a mass with radical equivariantly equal to N SM generations
    iff net copies are (N,N,0,0).

The forward direction is already proved. For the reverse, explicitly pair
matching representation/dual copies by evaluation, leave N copies of 10/bar5
unpaired, and prove the radical and its full-SM identification. Freeze the
precise carrier and generation representation types after source inspection.
The statement concerns existence of some mass, not every mass with those
counts. Include a degenerate-mass control illustrating that distinction.

Endpoint: the existence equivalence within this exact restricted family, or
an explicit obstruction to the proposed construction. It does not certify an
SU(5)-invariant UV action, a VEV realizing the mass, residual protection, or a
new protecting GUT. Those are separate questions. Do not add additional

Use the order above to obtain bounded reusable results before the difficult
all-degree support question. Work on this branch; optional bounded child
branches may integrate only here under the repository parent exception.

Each stage records results and counterexamples in separate short files linked
from the overview. Sources belong in Math, Physics or GUT by ownership, with
appropriate aggregate registration and persistent strict transitive audits.
Use focused builds, then a serialized full lake build at each coherent proof
milestone; check layout, local links and git diff. Commit verified checkpoints
and continue through the authorized stages unless a frozen stop condition or
user decision is reached. Do not use sorry, custom axioms, audit exceptions,
external arithmetic oracles or changed dependency pins.

The overall endpoint is a four-stage assessment with proved bounded results
and explicit remaining boundaries. If a stage needs a genuinely new physical
assumption or foundational project, record that boundary and continue other
independent authorized stages before returning for judgment. No wall-clock
completion estimate is asserted; uncertainty is concentrated in the actual
surplus realization, mass transport and all-degree support construction.

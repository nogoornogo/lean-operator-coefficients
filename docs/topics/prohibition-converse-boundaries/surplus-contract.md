# Frozen Surplus Counterexample Contract

Take a finite-dimensional nontrivial residual representation s, equal source
and target copy actions s in all three species, their actual external SM gauge
fields embedded in their direct product, and the invariant symmetric zero mass.
Construct every Species commuting square and the joint Realization. Prove that
all sources have no surplus over their equal targets for every tested residual
representation, while a physical participating light qque exterior coefficient
exists. A mass square, kernel split and actual coefficient certificate must be
proved, not supplied as assumptions. This is a degenerate-mass counterexample
within the original permitted mass scope, not an SM-spectrum realization or a
statement about full-rank masses. No new irreducibility premise is required.

## A reverse that is false

The raw charge filter that detects every formally selected B1 content is not
necessary. The existing all-degree lepton-parity input forbids B1, yet three
Q fields and no scalar have baryon grade 3 and zero lepton-parity charge.
The generic odd_coefficients_zero theorem proves that any odd fermion degree
has zero actual coefficients for all residual actions and scalar counts, using
Lorentz central parity. The neutral triple is therefore not an allowed operator.
neutral_b1_content and raw_filter_not_necessary record both sides formally.
This control is valid also with the constructed three-generation multiplicities.
It does not assert that all neutral contents vanish.

## Scope decision reached

The [contract](all-degree-contract.md) is complete at its support-aware endpoint.
A computable full q/l parameter classification would additionally require
certified nontriviality or zero-carrier tests for all relevant even-fermion
contents, with their gauge, Lorentz, permutation and finite-generation support.
Neither the degree-six dictionary nor the new guarded iff proves that support.
The existing bounded dimension-seven/eleven gates explicitly assume their
dimension bounds; they are not an all-degree cutoff.

Starting a new all-degree invariant-support classification would cross the
plan's foundational-project gate. Stop here and retain the known lepton-parity
certificate and all earlier bounded criteria. No new all-degree classification
of the 572 assignments or 151 generator orbits is claimed. Derivatives/EOM/IBP,
anomalies, quantum exactness and UV completion remain outside this topic.

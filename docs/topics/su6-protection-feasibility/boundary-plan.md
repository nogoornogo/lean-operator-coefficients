## Frozen Statement And Interpretation

Use the versioned [Angelescu et al. source](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.07366v2),
Sec. II, (4)–(10), and Appendix C. Build an algebraic boundary input from the
actual exterior-cube, exterior-square, fundamental and singlet carriers of
SU(6). Retain the standard physical SM group action. In repository ordering
the defining six has color, weak and singlet blocks of dimensions 3,2,1;
the paper lists the weak block before color.

The primary result is a boundary-selected light-field dictionary with a
genuine residual baryon action and prohibition of the specified B1 coefficient
domain. It must not assume the desired light charge table or identify a
boundary-selected subspace with a finite symmetric-mass radical.

Boundary values/projectors encode allowed chiral component amplitudes. The
unmixed boundary selection is constructed exactly. A statement about modes
after nonzero brane mixing must retain the corresponding equivariant
mode-identification premise unless independently proved. No claim about
analytic zero-mode profiles, the complete interacting KK spectrum, realistic
masses, quantum anomalies or all-orders quantum proton stability is included.

2. **Actual light components.** Realize the boundary conditions on the
   actual bulk carriers. The fifteen supplies left Q and ec, the twenty
   supplies right u, and the six supplies right d and conjugate L plus a
   neutral left singlet. The additional singlet's mixed boundary condition
   has no unmixed chiral mode. Prove the named embeddings, projections,
   inverse laws and actual SM-action correspondence. Reuse the existing
   exterior-square branching and derive the selected exterior-cube block
   structurally.
3. **Residual action and vertices.** Construct the color baryon phase as
   an SU(6) compensator times the central field phases with exterior degrees
   3,2,1,0. Use a harmless covering parametrization if needed to keep every
   character integral. Prove group laws, fixed supplied scalar/Higgs vacua,
   boundary compatibility and induced light charges. Treat right-handed
   conjugate fields with their inverse phases. Check the boundary bilinears
   and their scalar-dressed versions with the source's chirality convention.
4. **Coefficient connection.** Prove B1 vanishing on the constructed light
   SM/Higgs input. For jets and stable EOM/IBP quotients, prove the analogous
   scalar-action obstruction with its full-relation/selection hypotheses
   explicit; do not assume a selected coefficient extends globally. Explain
   exactly which statements are unconditional algebraic constructions and
   which require an analytic/mixed-mode certificate.
5. **Validation.** Check the source conventions, removed components and

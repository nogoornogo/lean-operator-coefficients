## Objective and scope

Make the mathematical origin and role of finite data understandable without
asking a reader to inspect hundreds of unexplained rational entries. Preserve
established coefficient-space statements, hypotheses, normalization and
literature boundaries. Kernel verification is necessary but does not itself
explain the intended operator dictionary.

The user authorized the [staged plan](plan.md), the
[bounded block continuation](exchange-block-plan.md) and autonomous continuation
through replacement. The [remaining-family plan](remaining-families-plan.md)
freezes subsequent Heeck–Le, finite-index, rank-example and bounded E6 phases.
Stop only at their stated resource, semantic or foundational boundaries; a
coherent milestone is not by itself a stopping point or merge authorization.

## Verified Li replacement

The [result](source-replacement-results.md) and
[verification](source-replacement-verification.md) close the original
selected-orbit gap. The [source derivation](source-derivation.md) connects actual
epsilon coefficients, factored Young actions, nineteen source amplitudes and
symbolic block extraction. Saved seed, forward-matrix and inverse-matrix tables
and their closed numerical certificates are retired. The full default build
and 73 fresh strict checks pass. Other certificate families are not thereby
claimed replaced.

Small index, scalar and polynomial checks remain. The target is unexplained
data and large closed computations carrying substantive mathematical
obligations, not every use of `decide`, `norm_num` or finite coordinates.
A tactic-name swap or a generated version of the same unexplained table does
not satisfy this objective.

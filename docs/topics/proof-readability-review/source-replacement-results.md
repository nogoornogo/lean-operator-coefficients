# Li source and selected-orbit replacement

The Li six-quark replacement closes the gap left by the earlier probe/block
milestones. The existing literal-basis, reconstruction, printed-span,
exchange, residual and flavor-boundary statements retain their hypotheses,
normalization and physical scope. The full default build passes with the new
proof route. See [verification](source-replacement-verification.md).

## What was retired

The active source no longer supplies the 25-by-25 forward matrix, 25-by-25
inverse or eleven 5-by-5 projected seed arrays as evaluated tables.
`basisRational` and `rationalSeedCoordinates` remain as compatibility names,
but are defined by actual-source recipes and selected exchange words.
The old rational inverse-product proofs, closed seed-probe certificate and
separate stepping-cache checker are removed. These were auxiliary certificate
interfaces; the coefficient-space and consumer results remain.

The literal permutation/weight dictionary is retained as the source convention.
A uniform identity on arbitrary six-slot functions equates it with the factored
Young action. This dictionary is distinct from a proposed evaluated rank or
inverse premise. Small permutation-index, epsilon and scalar checks remain;
this is not a claim that the repository contains no finite arithmetic.

## Mathematical route

The [source derivation](source-derivation.md) explains the detailed interfaces.
Raw color/spin coefficients are reconstructed by polynomial epsilon identities
on arbitrary vectors. Linear generator transport carries their Young images
to actual multilinear coefficients. Exact scalar-field transport supplies the
rational presentation without a cached coordinate agreement certificate.

Explicit coordinate changes split the local array into invariant 4-, 6-, 6-
and 9-dimensional product blocks. Uniform Young image formulas reduce each
nonzero block to a fixed direction and a scalar amplitude. Nineteen small
source-amplitude identities connect the eleven specified sources to those
formulas. The selected orbit columns are then constructed by their original
short exchange words.

The forward assembly agrees with the named block maps for arbitrary complex
assembly coefficients. Symbolic solvers reconstruct those coefficients. The
matrix inverse is induced by this extraction map; a generic finite square
matrix theorem turns the left inverse into a right inverse. A dependency audit
follows private helpers by owning module and rejects references to the retired
inverse, seed-probe and stepping premises.

The separating probe is half the trace of the first block. Every printed Young
image has zero such trace for arbitrary input, while the extra source gives
the normalized trace direction. The unchanged basis/span argument therefore
retains the 24-dimensional printed span and its physical down-flavor boundary.

## Remaining families and boundaries

This closes Li, not every numerical certificate in the project. The
[remaining-family plan](remaining-families-plan.md) freezes subsequent Heeck–Le,
SU(6), rank-example and bounded E6 phases on this branch. Their old certificates
must not be described as replaced merely because they reuse Li interfaces.
The resource limits and foundational stop conditions remain in force.
No merge or push is authorized.

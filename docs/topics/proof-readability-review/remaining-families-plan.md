## Heeck–Le six-quark certificate

Sources: [original topic](../heeck-le-sector-comparison/README.md),
[data](../../../Physics/StandardModel/Operators/HeeckLeSixQuarkData.lean),
[literal convention](../../../Physics/StandardModel/Operators/HeeckLeSixQuarkConvention.lean),
[certificate](../../../Physics/StandardModel/Operators/HeeckLeSixQuarkCertificate.lean),
[local basis](../../../Physics/StandardModel/Operators/HeeckLeSixQuarkLocalBasis.lean).

Preserve the two literal contractions, their normalization, the existing
selected sources/exchange words, local-basis statements, residual transport
and physical-sector hypotheses. Replace the projected seed arrays by their
separated color/spin formulas, construct forward columns from the words and
derive extraction through the established invariant product blocks. Do not
replace actual literal agreement by a statement about a newly chosen abstract
space, or import the old inverse into the new extraction proof.

An exact diagnostic decomposition suggests the following manageable route;
these ranks are proposals to formalize, not proof premises:

- Source 0 has no color-pair component, so its columns have zero 2-by-2 and
  2-by-3 blocks. Source 1 supplies those blocks. Its twelve selected
  coefficients give a ten-dimensional projection with two remaining directions.
- Five selected up-exchange pairs from source 0 split into five even and five
  odd directions. The remaining three source-0 coefficients and the two
  remaining source-1 directions give a five-variable coupling problem.
- Resolve the two- and three-factor blocks and that coupling symbolically;
  check the actual source connection before reusing any resulting inverse.

Freeze each concrete solver's formulas and uniform reconstruction statement
before testing it. Split scalar/component proofs when needed. If this route
fails, retain the exact obstruction and move to the other independently
bounded reviews; do not call the old numerical certificate replaced.

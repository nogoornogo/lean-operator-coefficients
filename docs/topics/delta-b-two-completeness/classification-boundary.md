# Six-slot invariant-classification boundary

## What is now internal

- Intrinsic exterior degree `(2,4)` restriction and reconstruction, including
  the exact factor `2! 4! = 48`.
- The `SSS` and `AAS` color tensors in the index convention of
  arXiv:1901.07519, their pair symmetries and their `SU(3)` invariance.
- The two-component Weyl Schouten identity.
- The exterior/statistics Fierz identity
  `O²_RRR - O¹_RRR = 3 O³_RRR` in repository conventions.
- Spanning of arbitrary six-right-Weyl invariants by five epsilon pairings.
- Spanning and unique five-coordinate reconstruction of arbitrary six-color
  `SU(3)` invariants. See [the structural proof](six-color-classification.md).
- Combined spanning on `Color ⊗ Spin` quark slots and unique 25-coordinate
  reconstruction for arbitrary intrinsic exterior restrictions. See
  [the bridge](color-weyl-bridge.md).
- The restriction's species sign rule, explicit constraints on the 25
  coordinates, and injectivity on intrinsic degree `(2,4)` coefficients.
- An explicitly reconstructed two-dimensional statistics kernel, two actual
  quark probes with both inverse identities, and the intrinsic degree `(2,4)`
  sector's linear equivalence with `ℂ²`. See
  [the final proof](two-coordinate-classification.md).

## What is not implied by those results

The operator constructions and Fierz identity show that the conventional
tensors produce elements of the desired invariant sector and satisfy the
expected relation. Alone, they do not show that arbitrary invariant six-linear
functionals are generated by those tensors. The standalone color and Weyl
classifications now supply that implication for their separate tensor domains;
the combined quark-slot domain is now bridged. The subsequent species quotient
and normalized alternating lift prove the full two-coordinate conclusion.

`SMEpsilonUniqueness.color_unique` classifies invariant trilinear forms on
three color fundamentals.  A six-linear invariant does not factor into two
invariant trilinear forms, so applying that theorem twice would be invalid.
Likewise, `WeylSpinorUniqueness.eq_smul_four` classifies the two-left/two-right
four-spinor case, not six equal-chirality spinors.

The user authorized internal six-slot invariant classification at stage 01.
The following alternatives describe the original decision boundary:

1. formalize the relevant first fundamental theorems for `SL(3)` and `SL(2)`,
   then derive the pair-symmetry quotients; or
2. perform and maintain complete coordinate classifications (up to `3^6`
   color and `2^6` spin basis values) with kernel-checked certificates; or
3. take the literature spanning assertion as an explicit theorem parameter,
   making the final coefficient theorem conditional; or
4. redefine the target as the contraction-generated image, which proves an
   inverse only for that image and is not completeness of the intrinsic
   invariant sector.

The selected internal route is now implemented independently for color and
right-Weyl tensors. The color proof reduces coordinates by pair partitions and
a common three-term relation; its rejected 85-step elimination draft is not a
dependency. No literature spanning assertion was introduced as a premise.

## Why the inverse is now unconditional

Two evaluations can prove independence of two constructed vectors and invert
their span.  They cannot prove that the intrinsic sector equals that span.
Adding the inverse before combined spanning would hide the completeness
premise. The proof now includes decomposition on actual quark slots, the
restriction's sign rule, necessity and sufficiency of the reduced equations,
and the normalized lift back to the original intrinsic sector. Consequently
the two probes classify that whole sector, not merely the span of two
constructed contractions. No rank-two assertion has been assumed.

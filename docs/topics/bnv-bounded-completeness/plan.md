# Staged Extension Plan

## Target Architecture

For a fixed bound D, define the full matter/Higgs BNV coefficient space C_D
independently of candidate contractions. Derive its finite species support;
for each supported type prove an invariant local dictionary and a complete
statistics-constrained residual Hom R_t. Establish

`C_D ≃ₗ[ℂ] ∏ t, R_t`.

Then prove `all coefficients vanish ↔ every R_t is zero`, with the dual
existential nonzero criterion. Direct sums and products are interchangeable
only after the finite support and the actual equivalence are proved.
Conjugate sectors may use semilinear transport; do not silently identify
complex-linear and conjugate-linear maps or assume residual conjugation data.

## Phase A: Through Seven

1. Pin conventions and an explicit weighted alphabet; inventory reusable APIs.
2. Construct the mixed-statistics independent coefficient input.
3. Prove species support and conjugate handling, including dimensions below six.
4. Reconnect all four dimension-six factors and prove each dimension-seven
   factor with explicit literal contraction and inverse certificates.
5. Assemble the entire bound-seven decomposition and total prohibition iff.
6. Verify controls, audits and documentation. Apply the
   [seven-dimensional acceptance gate](dimension-seven.md) before expanding.

## Historical Phase B: Through Eleven (Not Pursued)

The user decided at stage 01 not to pursue this phase. The inventory below
records the earlier proposal only; it is not a current implementation plan.
The original proposed acceptance rule was: for each milestone,
freeze its per-type inventory, required local invariant interfaces and actual
theorem inputs before implementation. A milestone is complete only after
species exhaustiveness, all local classifications and the enlarged bound's
whole-space inverse and prohibition criterion are verified.

| Dimension | New structural burden | Main reuse / proof obligations |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | Four fermions and two scalar insertions | Symmetric boson carriers, additional weak contractions, Bose/Fermi exchange compatibility |
| 9 | Six fermions, or four fermions and three scalar insertions | Existing specified six-quark result covers only one type; classify the remaining chiralities/species and mixed scalar types |
| 10 | Six fermions plus one scalar, or four fermions plus four scalars | Independent six-slot local dictionaries, mixed carriers, repeated boson relations |
| 11 | Six fermions plus two scalars, or four fermions plus five scalars | Complete all supported signed types and every constrained residual factor, then assemble C_11 |

These classes describe the reference's tables, not proved exhaustive support.
Do not deduce higher-degree weak or Weyl completeness simply by multiplying
known low-degree invariants: their relations and spanning need proof. Prefer
existing left/right factorization where available; develop bounded new local
classifications only when their statement and finite certificate route are fixed.

The final scope covers all baryon-violating charges within the stated alphabet,
not only Delta B = 1. The support theorem determines Delta B and Delta L.
Vanishing tests must retain any nontrivial Higgs residual action and all typed
flavor exchanges. Pairwise constituent overlap is not a substitute for the
complete tensor/quotient Hom criterion.

## Reference And Proof Strategy

Heeck–Le v2 is an expression/inventory cross-check, not the definition of the
complete input or a trusted enumeration oracle. Exact scripts may propose
finite basis matrices, relations and enumeration witnesses; Lean must check
them and connect them to the independent invariant space. A mismatch is a
valid documented result and does not permit weakening a gate.

No requirement to reproduce compact-term minimality, all tableau conventions
or the full flavor-count tables. Use independently verified coordinates even
where the source's simple compact contractions are nonminimal. Record literal
comparisons separately from abstract theoretical extensions in coverage ledgers.

## Verification, Checkpoints And Stop Conditions

Commit coherent verified phases on this branch; bounded child branches may
target this parent with the workflow exception recorded. No master integration
is automatic. At proof milestones run focused builds, persistent strict
transitive audits, full `lake build`, layout/link/diff checks and relevant exact
scripts. Maintain completed-result dossiers and verified assumptions without
editing the user's root README emphasis.

The current authorized endpoint is C_7's complete decomposition and total
prohibition/allowance iff, including the application controls. The C_11
extension will not be pursued under the closing decision. Dimension twelve is
an optional later decision, not a completion requirement or automatic task.
Stop for user judgment on a counterexample invalidating the target, new
physical species, a large unplanned foundation, new dependencies, an external
mathematics premise or a scope change. Routine bounded algebraic lemmas and
finite certificates within a frozen milestone do not require a new topic.

Excluded throughout: field-strength insertions, derivatives, EOM/IBP,
amplitudes, RGE, hadronic matrix elements, numerical dynamics, UV generation
and lifetime predictions. The final title and theorem must retain these
restrictions; an unqualified claim about all SMEFT BNV operators is not valid.

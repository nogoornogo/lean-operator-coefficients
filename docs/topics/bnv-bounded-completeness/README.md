## Endpoint And Result

The authorized dimension-seven gate is complete. The independently defined
matter/Higgs BNV coefficient space through seven is complex-linearly equivalent
to eight dimension-six and eight dimension-seven constrained residual factors.
Both baryon-number signs and all lower degrees are included. Extraction and
reconstruction are inverse; total prohibition holds iff every factor vanishes,
and a permitted nonzero coefficient exists iff some factor is nonzero.

The [completed proof dossier](../../proof/current/applications/Physics/BNV_SEVEN_COMPLETENESS.md)
records the inputs, proof and exclusions. A species-preserving equivariant
actual-field dictionary transports the entire input. Finite cyclic controls
prove complete prohibition and explicit nonzero allowance.

## Scope And Closing Decision

The alphabet is ten signed SM fermions, one Higgs doublet and its gauge dual,
with residual actions of arbitrary abstract groups and arbitrary multiplicity
representations, without topological or continuity assumptions. No physical
conjugacy between independent multiplicity modules is assumed. Field strengths,
derivatives, EOM/IBP, UV generation and rates are excluded. Heeck–Le supplies
comparison candidates, never a completeness premise.

On stage 01 the user closed this topic at dimension seven and decided not to
undertake the dimension-eight-to-eleven individual extensions. Those degrees
have arithmetic support bounds only, not coefficient completeness. The earlier
extension plan is retained as historical context, not pending work. The successor
direction is a purely algebraic general completeness theorem, not degree-by-degree
enumeration or an external computational generator.

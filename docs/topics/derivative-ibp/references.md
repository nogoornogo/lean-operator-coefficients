# Literature And External Source Register

This is a bounded pre-implementation survey, not an exhaustive prior-art search
or a source-to-Lean completeness certificate. No literature theorem is admitted
as a premise. Inspected locations and unverified bridges are distinguished below.
The later [scalar implementation](../../proof/current/core/Math/SCALAR_JET_IBP.md)
proves its own jet/divergence construction and complete quadratic consumer.
R1/R2 remain design guidance; no momentum-symbol, on-shell or paper-wide
counting comparison is certified by that result.

## R1 — Scalar Momentum-Space Pilot

B. Henning, X. Lu, T. Melia, H. Murayama, *Hilbert series and operator bases with
derivatives in effective field theories*.

- Pinned [arXiv:1507.07240v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07240v1);
  [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1507.07240v1).
- Inspected: Sec. 1, Eqs. (4)–(6), and Sec. 2, Eqs. (9)–(13).
- Role: fixed field multiplicities, symmetric momentum polynomials and the
  translation relation generated by the sum of momenta.
- Conditions: the worked system has scalar fields in one spacetime dimension;
  the discussion distinguishes IBP from kinetic EOM. Boundary assumptions
  enter the integrated interpretation.
- Limit: no Fourier/operator semantic equivalence, arbitrary-dimensional
  counting formula or EOM theorem has been checked in Lean here. The proposed
  IBP-only pilot must omit the paper's on-shell relations.

## R2 — Two Descriptions Of Derivative Operators

B. Henning, X. Lu, T. Melia, H. Murayama, *Operator bases, S-matrices, and their
partition functions*, JHEP 10 (2017) 199.

- Pinned [arXiv:1706.08520v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08520v1);
  [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.08520v1).
- Inspected: Secs. 2.1–2.2, especially Eqs. (7)–(9) and the field-redefinition
  discussion immediately before Sec. 2.2; Sec. 5.1 from Eq. (103), and the
  summary of momentum/EOM/Gram relations in Sec. 5.7.
- Role: compare position-space differential expressions with fixed-slot
  momentum polynomials, and identify where cohomology and on-shell inputs enter.
- Conditions: the paper's single-particle modules already incorporate linearized
  equations and its physical interpretation concerns perturbative amplitudes.
- Limit: neither on-shell modules nor an S-matrix equivalence may be silently
  used in an off-shell IBP-only extension. The paper's counting/conformal/Hodge
  results are not premises of the proposed first package.

## R3 — Limits Of Simple Counting Subtraction

L. Lehman, A. Martin, *Low-derivative operators of the Standard Model effective
field theory via Hilbert series methods*.

- Pinned [arXiv:1510.00372v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.00372v1);
  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.00372v1).
- Inspected: abstract, introduction, Sec. 2, Eq. (2.1), and its restriction
  discussion around Eq. (2.9) and Sec. 2.1.
- Role: guard against assuming that subtracting the number of vector
  structures always computes the IBP quotient dimension.
- Condition: the stated simple algorithm requires a unique Lorentz invariant
  per derivative partition; the abstract explicitly limits higher-derivative use.
- Limit: this survey does not audit the operator counts or tables, and does not
  treat the older algorithm's restriction as a limitation of all later methods.

## A/B Implementation Source Reconciliation

uses the pinned Physlib exterior even derivation directly and the attributed
local symmetric fragment to check the scalar free-algebra presentation bridge.

the larger external SM, Lorentz and analytic import closures remain excluded.

are now actual checked inputs to
`Math.LinearAlgebra.SymmetricPolynomialPower`. Its new equivalence connects
the symmetric tensor source to homogeneous polynomials and proves evaluation
on arbitrary vectors. The mixed source embedding is injective, its grade
restriction has an inverse, and its zero-derivative boundary recovers the
existing mixed coefficient source equivariantly. The
[new audit](../../../Math/Algebra/Audit/AuditMixedJetIBP.lean) checks those
connections and the complete consumer transitively.

injectivity and Clifford contractions. The contractions serve coordinate
extraction only. R1/R2 continue to guide scope; neither their EOM/counting
claims nor a literature completeness theorem is a premise. The bibliography
is reconciled with these sources without adding a comparison coverage row.

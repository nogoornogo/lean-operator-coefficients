# Two-Route Feasibility And Cost Assessment

This closes the authorized literature/library survey, not the future proof
endpoint. Both named routes were inspected once. No jet/IBP theorem was
implemented and no dependency change is proposed by this report.
The later user-authorized [dependency connection](dependency-integration.md)
supersedes that checkpoint's dependency choice; its result does not implement
the missing derivative/IBP semantic bridge.

The subsequent authorized [implementation](implementation-plan.md) selects
Route A and completes the [scalar foundation](../../proof/current/core/Math/SCALAR_JET_IBP.md).
The original proposed contract and survey evidence below retain their
checkpoint scope; the mixed-statistics and spacetime bridges remain separate.


## Finding And Correction To The Initial Estimate

The pinned project already has the quotient/Hom descent layer. The pinned
Mathlib has polynomial derivations and useful weighted gradings. Importantly,
the external Physlib snapshot also has general even derivations of exterior
and symmetric algebras, commutation and covariance. The two isolated general
files compile unchanged in the current environment and eight selected axiom
reports use only standard axioms; see [actual evidence](library-survey.md).

Thus an estimate treating all mixed-statistics differential algebra as new
work is too pessimistic. What remains expensive is the semantic bridge:
constructing the graded jet carrier and divergence relations, proving their
completeness and symmetry compatibility, then connecting the actual quotient
to the existing coefficient source. The external model-wide package is not
an already verified replacement for those obligations.

## Route A — Formal Position-Space Jets

Proposed algebraic source for finitely many scalar components and finitely
many derivative directions:

$$
A=\mathbb C[X_{a,\alpha}],\qquad
D_\mu X_{a,\alpha}=X_{a,\alpha+e_\mu}.
$$

The source uses infinitely many formal generator labels, but each expression
has finite support. Mathlib constructs a derivation from those generator
values. Field-count and total-derivative weights can be represented jointly
by its monoid-valued weighted-homogeneous submodules. For a frozen grade,
use a divergence map from the corresponding current space and take its linear
image. A schematic relation space is the sum of the images of D_mu on the
previous derivative grade; with spacetime transformations this must be
packaged as an equivariant vector-current divergence.

Existing local quotient/Hom descent can then be applied after proving
stability. No analytic integral or Stokes theorem is necessary for this formal
quotient endpoint. Claims about equality of integrated actions would require
an explicit evaluation/boundary theorem beyond it.

Advantages: close to Leibniz/IBP semantics; existing derivation and external
mixed-algebra lemmas are reusable. Main remaining inputs: generator indexing,
joint grading, current/divergence construction, action covariance and the
bridge to the existing exterior/symmetric coefficient presentations.

## Route B — Fixed-Content Momentum Symbols

With a fixed number of labelled slots, encode each derivative distribution
by monomials in p_(i,mu). The total-derivative symbol is multiplication by
P_mu = sum_i p_(i,mu). Translation relations are generated by these P_mu;
permutations of identical slots carry the statistics. R1/R2 motivate this
presentation, but their EOM relations must be omitted for an IBP-only pilot.

A purely algebraic symbol map on formal derivative tensors could supply the
correspondence; an analytic Fourier-transform theorem is not intrinsically
required. However, its inverse, permutation/statistics compatibility and
exact equality of relation spans must actually be proved. A quotient of
momentum polynomials alone is not that semantic bridge.

Advantages: finite variable set at fixed slot count, existing polynomial and
ideal machinery, and finite derivative-degree pieces. Main remaining inputs:
the symbol equivalence, normalization, repeated-field statistics and assembly
across field counts. Passing to contracted momentum invariants would add
Lorentz/Gram completeness; it is not needed in the first construction.

## Obligations That Neither Route Removes

1. **Fix the coefficient/source convention.** The current framework classifies
   invariant linear functionals on a source. Specify exactly what source is
   quotiented, and how its dual description corresponds to the chosen action
   coefficient presentation. Quotienting an operator space and quotienting a
   redundant coefficient presentation are not automatically the same map.
2. **Use the right relation object.** Total divergences in the full field
   algebra give a linear relation space, not automatically an ideal. In the
   fixed-slot symbol algebra, the multiplication is different and the
   translation relations can form an ideal. No contradiction or unrestricted
   identification of these algebra products is justified.
3. **Do not truncate by setting the highest derivatives to zero.** Work in
   the unbounded formal source and restrict graded maps, or use compatible
   truncation/embedding maps. D raises derivative order. Finiteness of a
   fixed grade must be proved; derivative weight alone leaves arbitrary powers
   of undifferentiated fields unless field count or positive engineering
   weight is also bounded.
4. **Retain actual symmetry data.** Internal residual actions may commute
   with D; spacetime transformations generally mix D_mu. IBP relates derivative
   distributions but preserves underlying species counts. Do not reuse the
   refined distribution decomposition unchanged.
5. **Avoid unjustified exactness.** For arbitrary groups, taking invariant
   vectors need not commute with taking a quotient. The existing Hom-descent
   route avoids asserting this unproved interchange. Counting formulas may
   need stronger hypotheses than the algebraic Hom criterion.
6. **Freeze normalizations.** Ordinary derivatives versus normalized Taylor
   jets and momentum-symbol phase/factorial choices change generator formulas.
   Finite matrix relations must be derived in the selected convention.

## Recommended First Proof Contract — Still Proposed

Prefer Route A for the formal IBP semantics, reusing the isolated generic
Physlib lemmas if adaptation is approved. Keep Route B as a later bounded
symbol comparison, rather than implementing both complete presentations now.
A useful first package has four independently checked gates:

| Gate | Output | Existing support; new obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Algebra | Scalar jet source, ordinary total derivatives and commutation | `mkDerivation`/extensionality exist; define jet shifts and prove the formulas. |
| Grading and action | Field-count/derivative grades and covariance | Weighted submodules and generic covariance tools exist; prove grade shifts and identify admissible current spaces. |
| IBP and coefficients | Actual divergence image, stability and inverse Hom correspondence | Quotient descent exists; derive the relation source and connect source/dual conventions. |
| Nontrivial consumer | A small fixed-content example with full relation span, surviving class and exact quotient description | Existing finite linear algebra helps; prove the concrete semantic dictionary and independence. |

A one-direction scalar example can test the construction; a four-dimensional
Lorentz-scalar consumer is a further gate, not established by merely adding
four direction labels. The initial proof contract should decide whether that
consumer belongs to the first package before implementation.

The mixed Bose/Fermi extension can reuse the externally checked even
exterior/symmetric derivations instead of rebuilding their core from scratch.
Its grading and existing-statistics/source bridge are still separate work.
General gauge-covariant derivative commutators, Bianchi/EOM relations,
amplitude equivalence, Hilbert-series formulas and arbitrary SMEFT counting
are not included in this first contract.

## Revised Cost And Stop Decision

The four gates are a planning decomposition, not a measured duration or a
promise of four sessions. The survey supports reducing the estimated cost of
the even-derivation core, but not a reliable calendar/token budget for the
whole extension. A scalar IBP foundation remains a multi-stage proof package;
full mixed-statistics integration is larger, and EOM/gauge/amplitude work is
a separate research programme.

The present survey stops here. Before implementation, freeze the source/dual
convention, grading, residual versus spacetime actions, first concrete consumer,
which external lemmas to adapt, verification gates and stop condition. No
whole external dependency or toolchain upgrade is needed merely to consider
those two generic files. No implementation authorization is inferred from the
successful source probes.

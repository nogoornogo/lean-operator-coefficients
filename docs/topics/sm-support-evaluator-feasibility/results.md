# Bounded feasibility result

## Decision

The subsequently authorized [cause isolation](diagnosis-results.md) identifies
occupation enumeration as a reproducible obstruction and narrows the next
recommendation to a compact count-triple enumerator. The initial experiment
and its evidence below remain unchanged.


Small closed structural support checks are usable now with local constructive
finite-decision instances and kernel reduction. A general compiled evaluator
and its large-content performance are **not established**. The first larger
Q18 probe fails at the existing kernel recursion limit; this is not a negative
support result. Stop under the frozen resource policy, without raising limits
or trying another algorithm. The full efficient-evaluator question remains open.

No production theorem, dependency pin, proof limit or active import changed.
The reusable [probe generator](../../../tools/structural-support/README.md)
stores diagnostic source templates, not a new library interface. Its successful
closed checks were actually elaborated and kernel-checked; the failed checks
are never accepted as proofs.

## What was tested

Route A found missing `Decidable` instances at the SU2, color and original SM
support interfaces. This is an interface gap, not undecidability. Occupation
cardinalities at total degrees zero and two compile to 1 and 6. Direct compiled
access to Q core metadata fails because
`SMSignedCoreJointPresentation.presentation` is noncomputable.

Route B used **one closed-data refinement**: elaborator normalization extracts
Q0/Q6 expression data, with `rfl` equalities back to the actual presentation.
The extracted expressions compile and have 1 and 20 branches, respectively.
The elaborator is not trusted for this correspondence: the equalities and
resulting proof terms are checked by the kernel. This is closed reflection,
not an executable function of arbitrary content.

Local owner equality and finite decisions for owner validity, diagrams, SU2
paths, joint words, expressions, content and Higgs support suffice for closed
kernel checks. No independent choices across color/weak/spin branches are
introduced. The generic content/Higgs decision instances remain noncomputable
for compiled execution. Even the LLHH Boolean `#eval` fails code generation.
No dynamic-programming or matching-pruning equivalence is claimed: the closed
refinement and existing exhaustive decisions were tested first and hit the
stop gate before implementing those larger refinements.

## Positive and negative evidence

The final `controls` probe contains 28 named kernel-checked results:
SU2 positive/negative paths; unit, pairing and determinant color support;
repeated-owner and adjoint failures, including reversed/dual cases; expression
zero; metadata equalities; LLHH and conjugate LLHH; the E^2 H^4 weak-bound
failure with neutral hypercharge; DUQL/QQUE/QQQL/DUUE; and the Q/conjugate-Q
independent-spin failure. In addition to Higgs predicates, it proves one actual
`OriginalSpace` allowance and one actual prohibition at k=1,q=l=0.

The Q6 original Higgs-support test at h=0,d=2 also passed `decide +kernel`.
The Q18 test at h=0,d=6 failed with:

```text
(kernel) deep recursion detected, use `set_option maxRecDepth <num>` to increase the limit
```

The suggested option was **not** used. Wrong positive and wrong negative SU2
candidates are separately rejected as false. The strict audit also rejects
Lean's error-recovery `sorryAx` in failed candidates; no source uses `sorry`
and no failed file is counted as a successful proof.

The manifest's unexecuted local controls (including conjugate metadata and
core overflow), local Q24 zero stress key and all 107 diagnostic rows remain
unverified here. Q18 did not pass the stage-3 gate, so no 107-row comparison was
run. In particular, no new 41/66 agreement or 572-assignment result is claimed.

## Next prerequisite and recommendation

A scalable evaluator needs a **uniform executable metadata/decision refinement**
proved equivalent to the existing joint predicate, rather than normalization
of individual closed goals. Its negative direction must certify complete search:
reachable-degree DP equivalent to CG paths and owner-level matching/triple
search equivalent to all labelled diagrams, with any pruning proved complete.
This is a separately reviewable implementation milestone, not a theorem
established by this experiment. Nothing here proves that such a refinement
will meet the resource bounds.

Recommend freezing that milestone around one fixed finite test-table domain
and its proved semantic bridge before attempting 107 rows or B1 searches.
A B1 solver additionally needs complete target-domain enumeration and a precise
assignment domain. The current failures do not challenge `original_ql_iff`,
prove impossibility of efficient evaluation or justify a new representation
classification project.

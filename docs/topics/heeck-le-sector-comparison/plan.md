# Frozen Comparison Plan

## Inputs And Claims

Reference: Julian Heeck and Brandon B. Le, *Basis for non-derivative
baryon-number-violating operators*, arXiv:2604.25134v2. Its broad dimension range
does not expand this topic's scope. The entries are unnumbered tables in
Section 6 (printed page 21) and Section 9.4 (printed page 23).

For each overlapping candidate list, express its literal contractions through
the existing independent-sector coordinate equivalence. Prove the image,
relations and inverse as appropriate. Distinguish independence of a raw list
from independence after field statistics and flavor relations, and distinguish
the number of local contractions from the dimension of flavor coefficients.
Numerical dimension agreement alone is not a candidate certificate.

Physical flavor identifications must be supplied/proved using existing
interfaces. Check physical right-handed versus left-conjugate charges, pairing
order, Grassmann signs, epsilon convention, alternatization normalization and
any symmetry-factor normalization. Do not add an unproved dictionary premise
and call its application a proof of the source statement.

## Milestones

1. **Source inventory.** Recover versioned full text/source with reproducible URL
   and checksum for downloaded inputs. Inventory DUQL, QQUE, QQQL, DUUE and the
   specified u²d⁴ entry. Record equation, page, flavor restrictions and exclusions.
   Read only relevant definitions, relations and entries. Where useful, compare
   v1/v2 for changes to these entries; v2 remains the target.
2. **Four-family dictionary and proofs.** Translate each dimension-six candidate
   separately. Reuse existing Li literal, coordinate and exterior certificates
   only where expression agreement is proved. Keep DUQL/DUUE and QQUE/QQQL
   certificate declarations distinct even if they share a source module.
   Certify spanning and independence in the actual constrained sector.
3. **Specified six-quark comparison.** First check the source's chirality/species
   match. Compare against the existing 25-local-coordinate typed-exchange space,
   not 25 free flavor coefficients. Separate one generation, up/down dimensions
   at most three, and arbitrary finite multiplicities. The known Λ⁴D boundary
   of the separate Li source must not be transferred to Heeck–Le without proof.
4. **Review and closure.** Audit all overlapping entries and record scope outcomes.
   Persist strict transitive axiom audits and aggregates for implemented proofs;
   run focused/full builds and layout checks. Maintain a short, separate English
   `docs/verification/heeck-le-coverage.md` at verified milestones, linking dossiers
   and this topic rather than copying investigations. Update applicable assumptions
   only for verified new results. Check links/diff and commit the bounded endpoint.

## Reuse And Resource Boundary

The available assets are the independently defined signed four-family sector,
the local Li candidate dictionaries, finite flavor coordinates and the specified
six-quark completeness/typed-exchange certificates. Residual symmetry remains
explicit where supported; a literature list with trivial residual action is
not silently treated as an arbitrary-H basis.

Bounded exact coordinate helpers and reproducible finite symbolic calculations
are acceptable; Lean certificates must not depend on trusting those calculations.
No automatic extension to other dimension-nine chiralities, dimension-seven
Higgs insertions, dimensions ten–twelve, derivatives, EOM/IBP, amplitudes,
anomalies, UV matching or rates. LLHH has ΔB=0 and is not a proposed overlap.
No AutoEFT installation or runtime output audit. Pins remain unchanged.

## Stop Condition

All five proposed overlaps have a documented, evidenced disposition and all
implemented comparisons pass the verification gates. A proved discrepancy is
a valid outcome, not a reason to widen the audit. Stop for user judgment if
matching requires a new physical premise, changing prior scope decisions, or
a large foundational project. Source unavailability must be reported honestly;
it does not count as a completed source audit. Master merge requires separate
authorization. No whole-paper correctness/error claim or coverage percentage.

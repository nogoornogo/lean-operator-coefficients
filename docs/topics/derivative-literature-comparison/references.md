# Pinned Sources And Inspection

These are primary sources. Version metadata and the selected PDF text were
inspected for planning; this is not a full-paper, visual-table or Lean audit.
Recheck literal indices, conjugations and normalization in the pinned PDF or
its TeX before transcribing a candidate. PDF pages below count from one,
including the title page; equation/table labels take precedence over pagination.
No literature theorem is imported as a premise. The
[execution results](results.md) and [coverage ledger](../../verification/derivative-interface-coverage.md)
now distinguish verified algebraic subsets from unproved physical/basis
bridges and supplementary diagnostic counts. This source inspection alone
does not certify the frozen [case](cases.md) targets.

## R1 — One-Dimensional Scalars

B. Henning, X. Lu, T. Melia, H. Murayama, *Hilbert series and operator bases
with derivatives in effective field theories*,
[1507.07240v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07240v1),
[pinned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.07240v1).

- Inspected: Section 2, Eqs. (2.6)–(2.9); Section 3, especially (3.6),
  (3.10) and (3.11), the latter on PDF page 13.
- Relevant result: fixed-multiplicity scalar counting in one dimension,
  separating kinetic EOM from IBP. Equation (3.11) gives the one/two-species
  generating functions used for C1's finite coefficient expectations.
- Conditions: commuting scalar fields, ordinary derivatives and free kinetic
  equations. The paper's momentum-polynomial ideal is not automatically an
  ideal of the complete position-space source algebra.
- Comparison boundary: finite actual jet quotients; no general Fourier,
  integrated-action or arbitrary-species Hilbert-series equivalence.

## R2 — Four-Dimensional Scalar Sector

B. Henning, X. Lu, T. Melia, H. Murayama, *Operator bases, S-matrices, and
their partition functions*,
[1706.08520v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08520v1),
[pinned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.08520v1).

- Inspected: Section 5.2's four-point construction and Section 6.1,
  especially Eq. (6.3), PDF page 58.
- Relevant result: the four-identical-scalar series has derivative-degree
  coefficients 1, 0, 1 at degrees 0, 2, 4.
- Conditions: massless linearized scalar equations and IBP, with the paper's
  Euclidean rotation-group presentation. A dictionary to the repository's
  complex Lorentz representation must be proved, not inferred from the count.
- Comparison boundary: C2's finite degrees; no S-matrix, conformal/cohomology
  or complete generating-function theorem.

## R3 — Warsaw Higgs Relation

B. Grzadkowski, M. Iskrzyński, M. Misiak, J. Rosiek, *Dimension-Six Terms
in the Standard Model Lagrangian*,
[1008.4884v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.4884v3),
[pinned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.4884v3).

- Inspected: Table 2 and Section 5's scalar four-field/two-derivative class,
  especially Eq. (5.3), PDF page 9.
- Relevant expressions: the two displayed derivative Higgs operators and
  the reduction involving Higgs kinetic, potential and Yukawa classes.
  Equation (5.3) uses schematic class notation; it is not a list of exact
  coefficients equal to one.
- Conditions: the source paper uses SM covariant derivatives and leading SM
  equations. C3 explicitly turns off gauge and Yukawa couplings, fixes a
  bosonic leading Lagrangian, and derives its own coefficients.
- Comparison boundary: that specialization and one cross-count relation;
  no certification of the full Warsaw basis.

## R4 — Dimension-Eight Higgs Candidates

C. W. Murphy, *Dimension-8 Operators in the Standard Model Effective Field
Theory*, [2005.00059v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00059v6),
[pinned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.00059v6).

- Inspected: Section 4.1 and Table 2, PDF page 20, the three operators in
  the four-Higgs/four-derivative class. Table 5, PDF page 23, supplies the
  right-handed charged-lepton/Higgs current for C5's second reading.
- Conditions: SMEFT covariant-derivative conventions and Hermitian/flavor
  counting. C4 first tests the explicitly free, gaugeless specialization.
- Version choice: v6's metadata records a correction to weak-doublet versus
  anti-fermion epsilon/Pauli pairing. Earlier-version table transcription
  is not silently reused.
- Comparison boundary: the specified Higgs class and optional shared current;
  no whole dimension-eight count, phenomenology or RGE claim.

## R5 — Mixed Weyl/Higgs Current

H.-L. Li, Z. Ren, J. Shu, M.-L. Xiao, J.-H. Yu, Y.-H. Zheng,
*Complete Set of Dimension-8 Operators in the Standard Model Effective Field
Theory*, [2005.00008v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00008v3),
[pinned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.00008v3).

- Inspected: Section 4.3.1, Eqs. (4.23)–(4.26), PDF pages 33–34. These
  include the charged-lepton-conjugate Weyl current times four Higgs fields
  and one derivative, and the two-/four-component current dictionary.
- Conditions: fixed chirality, field/conjugation order, derivative and flavor
  conventions. C5 fixes one flavor and states its free/gaugeless specialization.
  Equation (4.26)'s different current signs/orderings require a literal check.
- Version choice: v3 includes the stated revision to monomial
  desymmetrization. This is a different source from the repository's earlier
  derivative-free Li comparisons.
- Comparison boundary: one interacting field-content sector, not all flavors,
  all dimension-eight operators or the paper's full construction algorithm.

## R6 — First Order And Retained Higher Terms

J. C. Criado, M. Pérez-Victoria, *Field redefinitions in effective theories
at higher orders*, [1811.09413v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09413v2),
[pinned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.09413v2).

- Inspected: Section 3, Eq. (3.2), PDF page 10, and the following discussion
  of higher-order terms.
- Relevant statement: a perturbative field substitution has a first-order
  EOM contribution and additional higher-order terms.
- Conditions: C6 constructs its own finite polynomial example of that
  expansion. It does not formalize the paper's analytic functional,
  path-integral or observable-equivalence statements.
- Comparison boundary: exact coefficients through order two, with an actual
  first-order Euler/divergence identity. A nonzero raw second coefficient
  is not a proof that field-redefinition equivalence fails.

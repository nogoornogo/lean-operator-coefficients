## First Candidate: Babu–Gogoladze–Wang

*Gauged Baryon Parity and Nucleon Stability*,
[pinned hep-ph/0306003v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0306003v1),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0306003v1),
[correct publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2003.07.036).
The DOI in the supplied list differs from the arXiv publication metadata.

Inspected Sec. 2, Tables 1–2 and Eqs. (1), (4). Table 1 gives Z6 charges
(Q, uc, dc, l, ec, nuc, H) = (0,5,1,2,5,3,1) modulo six, using left-handed
fields. The selection combines discrete invariance with hypercharge neutrality:
2 Delta Y - Delta B + 3 Delta L = 0 modulo six. It excludes Delta B=1,2
and permits the Weinberg charge. This is not a three-way B1/B2/L2 prohibition.

Adapter inference: strongest first regression for simultaneous B1/B2
prohibition, with LLHH as a charge-neutral control. Actual nonzero LLHH needs
its separate existing coefficient witness. Higgs has nonzero discrete charge,
so the action is not the B/L-only character action of ComposableBLSupport.
Use the generic stable-specification interface and a discrete charge dictionary,
plus a proved local hypercharge-neutrality bridge. Translate opposite/conjugate
species explicitly. Right-handed neutrino inclusion needs a separate alphabet
extension; an SM/Higgs restriction would certify only that restricted sector.
Anomaly freedom, gauge origin and phenomenological rates remain external scope.

## Second Candidate: Dreiner–Luhn–Thormeier

*What is the Discrete Gauge Symmetry of the MSSM?*,
[pinned hep-ph/0512163v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0512163v3),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0512163v3),
[publication](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.73.075007).
Inspected abstract and Sec. 6 Eq. (6.1); v3 corrects a factor below Eq. (6.9).
The selected criteria retain B3/P6 and allow the seesaw operator. They are not
examples prohibiting Delta L=2. The candidate is useful for combined forbidden
and permitted supports, but chiral superfields, superpotential and Kahler
operators cannot be silently equated with the present fermion/boson input.
A component/statistics dictionary is an additional prerequisite. All-degree
claims must be checked separately from the listed low-dimension operators.

## Third Candidate: Mohapatra–Ratz

*Gauged Discrete Symmetries and Proton Stability*,
[pinned arXiv:0707.4070v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.4070v1),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0707.4070v1),
[publication](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.76.095003).
Inspected introduction and Sec. IV/Table IV, and conclusions. It studies
supersymmetric left-right, Pati–Salam and SO(10) settings with specified Higgs
content and symmetry breaking. Its proton-protection statements are not an
immediate certificate of arbitrary derivative-free B1/B2/L2 prohibition.
Residual action after VEVs and actual matter/light-field dictionaries must be
established before comparison. Defer behind the simpler low-energy candidate.

# SU(6) Exploration: Closure And Scope

## Decision And Documentation Role

On stage 01 the user chose to stop further SU(6) protecting-model exploration.
The recorded endpoint is a conditional obstruction, not the construction of a
protecting model or a general impossibility theorem. Broader classification is
deferred rather than scheduled as unfinished verification.

This record belongs to an independent model investigation, separate from the
current general operator-completeness claim. The topic's plans, results and
checkpoint evidence are retained under [topics](README.md); the reader-facing
[model study](../../model-studies/su6-protection/README.md) owns the application
summary. Stopping exploration does not make an application historical.
Reusable source files and current input interfaces remain available.

The working branch remains `proof/su6-twisted-protection`, with integration target
`master`. The formal generalization checkpoint is `checkpoint 01`. The user separately authorized master integration at stage 01.
Lean sources remain intact; branch cleanup follows the repository workflow.

## Retained Conclusion

The [scalar/block result](generalization-result.md) proves that every input in
its fixed standard-placement class admits a nonzero physical qque exterior
coefficient on participating actual light fields. Its genuine finite Abelian
matter action need not have a homomorphic SU(6) lift. Thus the specified residual
symmetry cannot prohibit every dimension-six Delta-B-one coefficient.

The class fixes the standard SM embedding, the `15 + bar6 + bar6` carrier,
pointwise scalar/block factors and the supplied benchmark mass invariance.
The [result's admission conditions](generalization-result.md#admission-conditions)
remain essential to its applicability. The earlier candidate calculations and
formal interface results retain their own, distinct verification boundaries.

No protecting model was found in the tested routes. This does not prove that
none exists for arbitrary SU(6) actions or light placements. An allowed invariant
coefficient is not a generated Wilson coefficient, decay amplitude or lifetime.

## Deferred Directions

Copy mixing, other mass forms or light placements, non-Abelian residual groups,
outer/antilinear actions and general flavor actions are outside the proved
class. They are possible changes of the classification problem, not missing
checks of the accepted theorem. None is asserted to provide protection.

These directions primarily require exact algebra or representation theory;
they do not inherently require numerical analysis. In the fixed placement,
Q, u^c and e^c all lie in the fifteen. Mixing only the two bar6 copies while
retaining the theorem's action on the fifteen and its participating light
species leaves the displayed qque cancellation intact. This observation is
not a classification of arbitrary mixed models.

Matching, generated coefficients and decay rates are separate physical
questions. RGE, nonperturbative QCD, hadronic matrix elements and large numerical
simulations remain outside this repository's formal endpoint. No new numerical
environment or broader search is planned.

# Remaining Patterns: Ordered Bounded Tests

The user authorized examining remaining patterns in sequence.

1. Pointwise inner lifts without a homomorphic lift: prove equality of their
   physical and gauge-removed coefficient actions on local coinvariants, and
   prove that the gauge-removal multiplication defect disappears there.
   Do not infer a commuting field representation or an old charge no-go from
   a projective field action.
2. Non-reversing actions: compare the actual induced coefficient action with a
   reference action. Prove precise equality/descent transport conditions under
   which an existing forbidden/nonzero result transfers. Record what remains
   unclassified when those certificates are absent.

The output is reusable coefficient-action tests, not a classification of all
SU(6) automorphisms or a proof that no protecting model exists. A full
non-split-extension classification and an actual protecting-model construction
are separate endpoints. Preserve the positive SM target, field/Lorentz/support
matching requirements, pins and strict audit allowances.

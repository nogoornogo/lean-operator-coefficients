# Statement And Convention Dictionary

The Stage A/B contract is the frozen plan specialized to the existing APIs.
No supplied generic stabilizer, rank identity, spanning or saturation premise
enters the implemented declarations.

## Actions And Domains

- `G` is the local group, `H` the larger residual group, `J` the smaller group,
  and `j : J ->* H` the comparison map. Injectivity/surjectivity of j is not
  required. For literal subgroup applications use its inclusion.
- `a/b` are local fermion/boson representations, `c/d` residual multiplicity
  representations; alphabets `P/Q` are finite. The fields are tensor products
  and the coefficient domain is exterior degree n tensor symmetric degree m.
- `allowed n m` is unchanged under the comparison and permutation-stable.
  Support selection and symmetry enhancement are different statements.
- `Protected n m` says every selected smaller-group invariant functional is
  invariant under the full original G x H action. Restriction retains its
  underlying functional. Its surjectivity is the substantive iff criterion.
- Charges range in an arbitrary additive abelian group A, allowing vectors
  of integral 3B/L and supplied flavor labels. An `AddChar A C` is evaluated
  at the summed charge. The actual field scalar action is an explicit premise.
  Generic charge labels alone do not prove that action premise.
- Linear flavor selectors remain separate from content support. A physical
  flavor basis or mass basis is not constructed here.

## All Degrees And Finite Bounds

All-degree functionals are products, equivalent to Hom out of the constructed
carrier direct sum. Finitely supported degree families are DFinsupp spaces.
Surjectivity and total prohibition agree degreewise in the two presentations;
this is not an equality of product and direct sum. All-bound protection uses
all natural cutoffs, with no uniform finite cutoff. No analytic series appears.

## Scalar And Dual Orientation

On a chosen ordered content, the domain scalar is the product of fermion and
boson slot scalars. It is derived on heterogeneous tensor powers, then on
`localCoinvariants tensor multiplicities`, then on the actual exchange quotient.
For an invariant functional f, invariance and linearity give z*f(v)=f(v).
Thus z != 1 kills f. In the usual contragredient coefficient action the
scalar would be z inverse; its neutrality condition is the same. The proof
uses functional invariance directly and introduces no incorrect dual charge.
No converse from charge neutrality to invariant existence is asserted.

## Polynomial Evaluation

The separate evaluation interface uses coordinate polynomials
`J -> MvPolynomial I C` and supplied linear actions on `I -> C` and `J -> C`.
The construction accepts arbitrary coordinate sets; finite I/J are the intended
finite-dimensional specialization. With infinite J, it is coordinatewise
polynomial data, not a claim of a single uniform polynomial degree.
Covariance is independently checked at every g and value. Evaluation lands
in the fixed space of the actual stabilizer of that value.

The one-coordinate weight-one example is holomorphic; its nonzero-value
surjectivity is proved by a degree-one polynomial. It does not certify the
conjugate-spurion MLFV dictionary, a generic orbit theorem, or UV generation.
At zero the stabilizer changes; evaluation into the ambient space vanishes.
This is not a counterexample to saturation into the actual zero fixed space.

# Frozen Staged Plan

Planning date: stage 01. This is a prospective plan, not a proved result.

## Common Contract

Use finite fermion/boson species alphabets and supplied complex local/residual
representations, independently defined coefficient domains
`Lambda^n Vferm tensor Sym^m Vbos`, and permutation-stable content selection.
Retain the coefficient-space decomposition hypotheses; add finite dimension or
semisimplicity only to explicitly named specializations that need them.

Write K for imposed residual symmetry, E for a proposed enlarged symmetry,
and G_local for the fixed local group. Compare K <= E on the same fields with
compatible actions and support; do not confuse local-group changes with
already proved residual restriction. Extra flavor symmetries must commute
with the local action and preserve the admitted field structure.

Charges are additive signed labels, not invariance requirements accidentally
imposed on violating operators. Use integer 3B and L, or a supplied abelian
charge group, including opposite charges on conjugate fields. Specify which
bosonic species, conjugates and any separately admitted field-strength
representations are present; the generic theorem does not itself supply a
physical field-strength dictionary. Fix flavor identifications or a flavor
symmetry before defining LFV. A single off-diagonal dipole is not all LFV.

All-degree algebraic functionals form a product (dual of a direct sum).
Polynomial interactions have finite degree support. Prove their prohibition
comparison; do not claim these spaces are equal or introduce convergence.

## Stage A — General Protection Interface

Define protection as every K-invariant coefficient in the selected domain
being E-invariant. First freeze whether each statement concerns all selected
coefficients or the vanishing of an independently specified forbidden support.
Under restriction-compatible support, characterize protection by surjectivity
of the natural injective E-to-K coefficient restriction, degree by degree.
Transport this criterion through constructed residual-Hom completeness, with
commuting extraction/reconstruction maps; prove the all-degree formulation.

Keep general linear flavor selectors separate from species-content support.
For additive flavor charges, prove the support dictionary. For arbitrary
selectors, use zero of their restriction to the invariant coefficient space;
no physical mass-basis identification is inferred.

Acceptance: exact iff statements, bounded/all-degree compatibility and a
control where subgroup restriction is injective but not surjective. Reuse
existing naturality; do not merely rename an existing vanishing predicate.

## Stage B — All-Degree Character Selection

For one content on which an admitted residual element acts as a scalar
character chi, derive the scalar on the tensor domain, show its action passes
through gauge coinvariants and signed/unsigned exchange quotients, and prove
that a nontrivial scalar forces every invariant functional to vanish.
Track contragredient coefficient orientation explicitly.

Package the result uniformly over arbitrary degrees and forbidden supports.
Include continuous-character and finite-cyclic specializations if the existing
APIs admit them without additional foundations. State congruence neutrality
as necessary; do not infer a nonzero coefficient from neutrality alone.

Acceptance: mixed-statistics bridge, simultaneous B/L/flavor charge support,
zero-degree and conjugate-charge controls, and a neutral content whose local
invariant space is zero. No fixed finite cutoff proves the all-degree claim.
Kobach/Helset comparisons require their exact SM/flavor dictionaries; do not
claim their full statements from abstract charge bookkeeping alone.

## Stage C — Bosonic Friendship Feasibility

Reference R1, Secs. 4.1–4.3, with its explicit fermionic caveat. Run one
feasibility pass over two routes: (C1) representation occurrence/branching
criterion on symmetric powers; (C2) generic-stabilizer/covariant-rank theorem.
Separate an elementary conditional implication from proof of its premise.

Freeze finite dimension, reductivity/compactness, representation category,
field/conjugate conventions and the meaning of generic stabilizer before
implementation. If these require new foundational milestones, record the
missing declarations and stop this stage for a scope decision. Do not port
the bosonic theorem to exterior or mixed powers by analogy.

Acceptance: either a proved precisely scoped bosonic criterion, or an explicit
feasibility decision with necessary hypotheses and missing foundations.

## Stage D — Spurion Evaluation Feasibility

Reference R2 Sec. 2.2 and R3/R4. Specify commuting spurion/dynamical actions,
polynomial versus conjugate-polynomial convention, evaluation value s and its
actual stabilizer K_s. Construct equivariant covariant evaluation into W^K_s
and prove image containment before asking whether it is surjective.

One pass over two routes: (D1) explicit finite algebraic examples with
constructed evaluation witnesses; (D2) the generic covariant theorem under
fully specified hypotheses. Audit genericity, orbit conditions and exceptional
values. Module rank equality alone is not evaluation surjectivity at s.
Arbitrary spurion degree and arbitrary operator degree are separate axes.

Acceptance: proved evaluation interface and either bounded exact saturation
examples or a documented general-saturation feasibility stop. Do not introduce
an unproved saturation premise as an established result or open a UV matching
project. Additional foundational scope requires a recorded user decision.

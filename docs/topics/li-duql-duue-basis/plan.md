# Verification Plan And Stop Gates

DUQL and DUUE own separate Lean certificate modules, strict audits and proof
dossiers (`LI_DUQL_BASIS.md` and `LI_DUUE_BASIS.md`), following QQUE/QQQL.
Only the topic record and final four-family composition are shared.

## 1. Recover And Freeze The Dictionary

Inspect the pinned v2 PDF for DUQL and DUUE. Record exact equations and pages
only after inspection; do not infer them from field labels. Specify paper and
repository orders, normalized bracket pairings, gauge epsilons, chirality,
dagger/dual identifications and permutation signs. The paper supplies candidate
data, never a trusted completeness premise. Freeze this dictionary before
implementing candidate maps.

## 2. DUQL Literal Exterior Certificate

Reuse `DuqlOrderedCompleteness` and `DuqlExteriorCompleteness`. Define the
printed ordered contraction independently of the inverse equivalence, prove
agreement on pure tensors and then through distinct-species exterior descent.
Prove its normalization and two-sided residual coordinate certificate.

## 3. DUUE Literal Exterior Certificate

Reuse `DuueOrderedCompleteness`, `DuueExchange` and
`DuueResidualCompleteness`. The existing exchange solution leaves one
unrestricted residual Hom, not a symmetric-U Hom. Identify how the printed
contraction realizes that solution, prove repeated-U signs and normalization,
and prove actual exterior candidate agreement with the inverse.

## 4. Finite Coordinates And Controls

For supplied finite flavor bases, transport the exact residual classification
to nonredundant coordinates. Retain H-equivariance as necessary and sufficient
linear equations; matrix/tensor units give an unrestricted basis only for
trivial residual action. Prove both inverses, independence, spanning and
cardinality. Check one-generation, empty-carrier and three-generation cases.
Use exact Lean controls for signs and normalization; any external computation
is supplementary and must have documented reproducible inputs.

## 5. Four-Family Composition

Compose DUQL/DUUE with the verified QQUE/QQQL candidate maps and
`DeltaLOneCoefficientCompleteness`. Prove that the direct product candidate
reconstructs the independently defined full signed-field sector and that
extraction is its inverse. Do not assume the four families as input when the
existing support theorem derives them. Counts must use compatible carrier,
chirality and conjugation conventions; no conjugate doubling by default.

## Acceptance And Stop Condition

Finish when literal contraction agreement, both family inverses, finite basis
certificates and four-family product agreement are proved and audited, with
focused/full builds, layout and documentation checks passing. Commit coherent
verified milestones. Completion does not authorize merging into master.

Stop for user judgment if the published family does not match the independent
sector under the stated dictionary, if completeness requires a new external
premise, or if a new foundational project, dependency change or physical scope
expansion is needed. Record a discrepancy rather than adjusting assumptions
silently. General p/y/m/j algorithms, higher dimensions, derivatives, amplitudes,
EOM/IBP, matching, RGE and rates are excluded.

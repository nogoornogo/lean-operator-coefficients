coordinates and their inverse. Under arbitrary residual action retain exact

of the invariant sector. Under trivial residual action prove independence,
spanning and dimension `n*(n+1)/2`, including empty, one-generation and
three-generation (six coefficients) controls. Check off-diagonal normalization
and a nontrivial order-two character against the existing nonzero criterion.

two-sided certificate, finite-coordinate/counting controls and existing VEV
agreement pass the acceptance checks. Commit coherent verified milestones;
master integration requires separate user direction.

under the stated conventions, if a new literature premise or foundational

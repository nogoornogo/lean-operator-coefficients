## Dev–Mohapatra Dictionary

The SM/Higgs restriction assigns odd parity to all quarks, even parity to
leptons and the SM Higgs. Conjugate quarks remain odd. With repository b=3B,
the character is b modulo two. Absolute B=1 has b=±3 and is detected at every
degree. The proof applies to arbitrary supplied complex copy spaces.
The LLHH consumer uses one neutral lepton copy and the existing Weinberg
SM interface with neutral Higgs.

The six-quark witness uses two copies for both up and down flavor spaces.
Slots 0,2 are up flavor 0; down slots 1,4 have flavor 0 and 3,5 flavor 1.
A flavor covector summing the two coordinates pulls back a nonzero admitted
local coefficient. Its projectors commute with color/Lorentz actions, six
odd residual phases multiply to one, and the existing within-species sign
relations are preserved. The resulting selected evaluation is nonzero.
This proves allowance in the specified local/statistics/residual domain.
It does not certify the source antisymmetric down-flavor coupling, its
printed contraction or the generated coefficient formula. Full flavored SM
intertwiner transport is not an additional theorem of this consumer.

The full source alphabet contains inert eta, which is odd. The source's
eta L udd interaction illustrates why SM/Higgs-only B1 prohibition must
not be extended to that alphabet. An absent eta VEV is a separate physical
input and is not a coefficient-vanishing theorem.

## Ma et al. Dictionary

The selected right Weyl singlet N and complex scalar chi2 both carry the
printed residual phase omega. A bare pair has phase omega², detected by the
residual generator. The dressed pair has phase omega³=1. The Lorentz epsilon
form descends to the exterior square, and its dressed intertwiner evaluates
to one on the two spin basis vectors and scalar 1. These are actual actions
and coefficients, not only arithmetic charge checks.

No identification of this selected N with every nuR charge, full quark

cosmological result is asserted. Unlike the quarticity adapter's real eta,
chi2 is complex here. See the [source register](references.md) and

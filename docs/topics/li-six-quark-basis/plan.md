# Plan And Stop Gates

All five stages are complete for the approved extended epsilon-orbit endpoint.
The original ten-only spanning target was replaced after the recorded scope
gate and user approval. See [results](results.md) for the verified statements;
the extension does not claim a full Schur-factor decomposition or an exact
four-component / hook-length normalization dictionary.

## 1. Source Selection And Correspondence Gate

Inspect at most two named routes: Li et al.'s dimension-nine SMEFT basis
(arXiv:2007.07899), then one explicitly recorded alternative published six-quark
basis if needed. Pin the chosen version and visually inspect the relevant
formula/table. Record field content, chirality, color/spin contractions,
flavor symmetry, dagger interpretation, slot order and coefficients.
The generic-EFT 2022 paper motivates the method; it need not contain the
specific six-quark data required here.

The gate passes only if the candidate can be compared with the specified
physical sector and adds general-flavor exchange verification beyond renaming
the known two scalar basis vectors. Stop with a documented feasibility decision
if no such candidate is recovered, or if new physical identifications need
user judgment. Do not manufacture a literature basis from the existing inverse.

## 2. Literal Local Candidates

The approved bounded exploratory decomposition reached a rank discrepancy;
see the historical [rank investigation](rank-investigation.md).
The user approved the arbitrary-multiplicity extension: verify the additional
alternating-down / symmetric-up component, then an extended local basis and
its transport through the existing typed-statistics Hom. Do not restrict down
dimension or attribute the added component to the paper. Stop if this extended
finite certificate fails or a new mathematical/physical premise is needed.

Define the recovered contractions independently of the final completeness
inverse. Prove their evaluations in the existing five-color by five-Weyl
coordinate system. Check slot signs, bracket relations and all normalization
factors. A dependent or redundant printed list is acceptable only with explicit
relations and a certified nonredundant coordinate interpretation.

## 3. General Typed Flavor Certificate

Use independent up/down carriers in the dependent `(U,D,U,D,D,D)` slot family.
Derive the simultaneous flavor/color/Weyl exchange equations for the candidate
coordinates and prove necessity and sufficiency. Retain H-equivariance.
Construct literal assembly and extraction with both inverse identities against
the existing constrained residual Hom and physical exterior sector. Do not
substitute arbitrary unconstrained 25-tuples for the admissible coordinate space.

## 4. Normalized Exterior And Scalar Consumers

Reuse the established species-projection/alternating lift, including its 1/48
normalization, and prove actual candidate agreement. Transport through supplied
actual-field `Inputs`. Specialize to trivial scalar flavors and establish
agreement with the two probes, conventional basis and dimension two. General
flavor counting is included only if directly supported by the recovered finite
coordinate certificate; no universal dimension-two assertion is permitted.

## 5. Acceptance

Review literal source correspondence, both inverses, admissibility conditions,
signs and assumptions. Register sources/audits and run focused builds, strict
transitive axiom audits, full `lake build`, layout/link checks and diff checks.
Write the proof dossier from the proved endpoint's perspective and update the
assumptions ledger at verified milestones. Commit coherent checkpoints.

## Stop Condition And Non-Goals

Finish at a verified literal general-flavor certificate and scalar specialization,
or at the source gate's documented feasibility outcome. Stop for a scope decision
if a new external premise, foundational project or dependency change is required.
Do not expand to dimension-nine SMEFT as a whole, all six-quark chirality patterns,
general p/y/m/j construction, derivatives, EOM/IBP, amplitudes, UV matching,
RGE, hadronic matrix elements or rates. Completion does not authorize merging.

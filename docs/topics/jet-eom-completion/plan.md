# Frozen Contract

## Phase 1: Mixed Kernel

For arbitrary complex linear odd/even seed maps on the existing actual jet
modules, prove that the kernel of `LinearJetAlgebra.projection` is exactly the
two-sided ideal generated by odd and even insertions of every polynomial
derivative multiple of each seed. Establish the same statement with all vectors
in the linear relation modules as generators. Do not ideal-close the IBP range.
No finiteness, covariance or equation-homogeneity assumption is needed here.

Primary route: choose linear splittings of quotient-generator maps and extend
them to the free exterior/symmetric factors. Prove reconstruction modulo the
generated ideal by generator induction. Alternate route: universal maps out of
the congruence quotient. These routes concern the same actual projection.

## Phase 2: Four-Dimensional Complete Null Example

Use the actual free massless left-Weyl source and dual Lorentz directions in
four dimensions, with the existing spectator. At field content one odd and
zero even, prove that all positive-derivative image grades are divergence
ranges, hence the complete EOM/IBP source is zero. Give its dimension, empty
basis and zero invariant coefficient space, with the actual Lorentz action.
Connect a representative known to be nonzero after EOM to its zero IBP class.
The bounded consumer is derivative order one; a uniform higher-order theorem
is allowed if it follows from the same multi-index predecessor construction.
This is a complete null example, not a table for multi-field interactions.

Primary route: expand the existing mixed-word spanning generators and remove
one derivative from a positive multi-index. Alternate route: finite first-jet
coordinate expansion for the order-one consumer. Do not assume a rank table.

## Phase 3: Right/Conjugate Weyl And Adjoint

Construct complex coefficient conjugation on component-labelled ordinary jets,
with commuting shifts and involution. Define the right equation as the actual
conjugate of the fixed left seed (complex-linear after conjugating its equation
parameter), state its coordinate signs, and prove covariance for the existing
right spin action and the same real four-direction action. Transport the full
descendant relations and quotient shifts in both directions. On the paired
left/right odd source, construct the semilinear involution swapping chirality;
extend via the existing exterior Hermitian conjugation, including product-order
reversal and involution. Prove compatibility with derivatives and the actual
linear divergence range, and descend the adjoint to EOM/IBP. A free scalar may
remain an external spectator; extending the adjoint to arbitrary even systems
is not required. Do not identify this algebraic source adjoint with an analytic
Hilbert-space adjoint or a raised/lowered classical field-index convention.

Primary route: coefficientwise conjugation and the existing exterior adjoint.
Alternate route: the same maps into conjugate scalar modules. A missing
theorem-level prerequisite after both routes is a documented feasibility stop;
do not add covariance premises to the physical consumer or start new foundations.

## Common Acceptance And Stops

Keep dependency pins, default heartbeat/recursion/kernel limits and old proofs.
Use four Lean threads, a 540-second command limit and inherited 16 GiB
per-process address-space guard for heavy checks. Persist strict transitive
axiom/import audits, register all source files and audits, run focused builds,
one serialized full `lake build`, layout, local links, topic index, metadata
and diff checks. Review statements, assumptions, source roles and limitations;
commit verified milestones and continue through all three phases. A checkpoint
is not a stop. Stop on completion of all gates, or an actual failed pair of
named routes requiring changed scope, authority or independent foundations.
No master integration, remote mutation, interacting EOM, gauge commutators,
Maxwell/Bianchi theory, rates or numerical dynamics are included.

# Two-Slot Young Foundation

## Frozen endpoint

For an arbitrary complex representation rho and an equivariant involution t,
construct the signed projector P_s = (id + s t)/2 for s^2 = 1. Prove an actual
equivariant equivalence from the quotient generated by t(u) - s u to the
s-eigenspace. Prove the full decomposition into the +1 and -1 components,
and specialize to the exchange of two tensor slots. No finite dimensionality,
compactness or chosen basis is required.

## Result

All statements above are proved. In addition, coefficient classification
transports from the signed relation quotient to Hom from the actual signed
component. The tensor specialization gives the normalized symmetric and
antisymmetric pure-tensor formulas and the vanishing antisymmetric diagonal.
The shape labels (2) and (1,1) refer only to the two S2 components; no general
Young tableaux construction, Schur-Weyl duality or basis enumeration is claimed.

The route replaces the earlier Hom-only comparison at the level of these
signed relation quotients: the input representations themselves are now
equivalent. The subsequent carrier adapter now identifies independently defined
symmetric and exterior squares with the eigenspaces and splits tensor square
into those standard carriers. Larger symmetric groups remain future work.

## Independent square carriers

[TwoSlotYoungSquares](../../../Math/RepresentationTheory/TwoSlotYoungSquares.lean)
uses the existing symmetric/exterior universal properties. Embeddings send
product and wedge generators to the normalized signed projectors. Inverses
restrict the standard product/wedge projections. Both inverse laws are proved
on arbitrary inputs by generator extensionality; equivariance holds for every
residual group element. `splitting` gives `F tensor F ≃ Sym² F × exterior² F`.
The signed relation quotients also have direct carrier equivalences to these
standard squares. No basis or finite dimension is required. This is not yet a
p-basis or a Schur decomposition for three or more slots.

# Distribution and attribution

Project-owned source and documentation use the [Apache License 2.0](../../LICENSE).
Dependencies and third-party material retain their own licenses and applicable
notice and attribution requirements. Preserve required accompanying notices.

An earlier cleanup removed a local header and bundled license text while
retaining technical mathlib attribution. That cleanup did not establish an
absence of third-party obligations or revoke earlier grants. The bounded
[direct-reuse review](../topics/mathlib-direct-reuse/README.md) records technical
provenance; it is not a repository-wide legal audit.

Topic records for obsolete tooling trials, local scratch cleanup and purely
editorial reorganizations are omitted from the maintained documentation.
[Workflow](WORKFLOW.md), [proof conventions](../proof/TEMPLATE.md) and the
[current result guide](../proof/current/RESULTS_AND_SCOPE.md) own the relevant
current guidance. Technical verification, dependency and provenance records
remain when they help readers understand or reproduce a result.

# SU(6) Protection Without Strict UV Commutation

## Question And Outcome

Can relaxing strict commutation with UV SU(6) allow a residual symmetry to
prohibit dimension-six Delta-B-one coefficients in the original model setting?
The investigation tested covariant actions and concrete candidates, then proved
a conditional obstruction for a specified scalar/block class. The separate
[boundary construction](boundary-model.md) now supplies algebraic protecting
amplitudes, with analytic mixed-mode admission explicit. The earlier exploration
was stopped at stage 01 and reopened for a bounded light-placement screen at stage 02. This application remains separate from
the general completeness claim.

## Limits And Reading

This does not exclude arbitrary SU(6) compensators, masses, embeddings or light
placements. A nonzero allowed coefficient does not establish a generated
interaction or a decay rate. Anomaly cancellation, quantum exactness and all
scalar sectors are not supplied. The unexamined symmetry directions primarily
require exact algebra or representation theory; phenomenological matching and
rates are separate questions.

Read the [result dossier](scalar-block-obstruction.md) for the theorem class and
actual-field construction. The [topic](../../topics/su6-twisted-protection/README.md)
retains plans and verification; [closure](../../topics/su6-twisted-protection/closure.md)
records the stopping decision and deferred directions. No broader search is planned.

# Independent Species Surplus Recheck

Keep N=1 and the residual action from
[1304.0576v2, Table I and Eqs.(11),(12)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.0576v2).
On the surviving baryonic order-18 element, all-left Q and uc copy weights are
2 and 16. The exotics are colorless and add neither species. Any common
irreducible with a nonzero map into each would have to carry both distinct
scalar actions, so no shared R exists. This obstructs the new inlet before
choosing any mass; permitting independent full sources/targets cannot repair it.

## Include The Charged Exotic Copies

For the allowed choice Y1=-1/2,Y2=-1, etaR^c is another copy of the ec SM gauge
representation, and etaL is its opposite gauge partner. In units b=6B modulo18,
both all-left exotic entries have weight9. Thus the *complete gauge-species*
ec source weights are {0,9} and the copy target weights are {9}; the surpluses
are 1 for R weight0 and 0 for R weight9. They still share no constituent with
both Q and uc. The earlier SM-selected ec action was not the entire gauge
species space. Opposite hypercharge alone does not give an SM-electron partner
with matching residual action.

The established physical SM qque prohibition remains unchanged; the charged
exotic correction does not change its external-field dictionary. Neutral
Majorana parameters cannot alter the Q/uc scalar conflict. The exact checks
remain conditional on supplied charges, with full stabilizer/quotient descent
outside scope. See [cross-model result](../../topics/species-surplus-model-checks/result.md)
and [verification](../../topics/species-surplus-model-checks/verification.md).

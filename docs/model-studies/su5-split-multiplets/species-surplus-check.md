# Independent Species Surplus Recheck

Using the supplied branching of [1706.08535v2, Eq.(1)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08535v2),
the complete Q/uc/ec sectors have independent source/target counts 3→2, 3→2,
1→0. For H=1 the trivial irreducible line has Hom surplus 1 in every species.
Source semisimplicity is automatic. All opposite gauge irreducibles are collected
in the partner sector; the other irreducibles and same-side sectors cannot pair.

Thus [the new generic theorem](../../topics/species-copy-surplus/result.md)
applies directly to arbitrary original SM-invariant symmetric masses on the
supplied carrier, deriving actual light kernels and physical qque permission.
This removes the previous need to first choose light lines and then fit them
into common S,T. The Q/u kernels may differ, have dimension greater than one,
or include 10 components. Pure exotic light-origin conditions remain a separate
UV tuning question.

## Full Parity And Light Parity Must Be Distinguished

The selected light action (+,+,-) has no common irreducible R. However, full
Eq.(22) has Q/uc source parity (odd,even,even), even partner copies, and odd ec
with no partner. The odd R therefore has surplus (1,1,1). If that full action
were exact for the mass, the 10's Q/uc columns could not mix with the even
partners and would survive alongside ec: qque on these odd components is even.

The actual exceptional mixing terms do not obey that full parity, as stated
in [Sec.V, Eq.(22)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.08535v2). Hence this positive
full-parity case is a changed mass constraint and light placement; it cannot
be substituted for the paper's selected even-quark light spectrum. The negative
light-parity case is not an invariant full-original-mass input.

This is a conditional algebraic application on literature-supplied SM sectors,
not a new Lean 40/50 branching or full UV adapter. No generated coefficient or
contradiction with the paper's suppression mechanism is inferred.

and [verification](../../topics/species-surplus-model-checks/verification.md).

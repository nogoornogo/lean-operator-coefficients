# Ma scotogenic model: SM external-field restriction

The fixed source is [hep-ph/0601225v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0601225v1),
Eqs.(3)–(6). SM fields and the usual Higgs are Z2-even; the additional singlet
fermions N and scalar doublet eta are odd, with eta assigned zero VEV.

On the original SM/Higgs external-field alphabet this Z2 action is trivial.
The Lean consumer proves B1 allowance with minimum derivative-free dimension six and actual
LLHH allowance. This distinguishes symmetry permission from the origin of a
coefficient: it does not assert that this model generates a baryon-violating
interaction or prove its radiative neutrino mass formula.

Parity checks confirm L eta N is even, L H N is odd, and LLHH is even. The
new N/eta alphabet, loop coefficient of Eq.(11), and its correspondence to a
registered LFV dipole map are not formalized in this pass. A future algebraic
consumer can accept such a coefficient map explicitly; its physical matching
is not established by the existing SM restriction.

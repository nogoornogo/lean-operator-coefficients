## What Is Checked

supports uR dR dR antiL Hdagger and Q Q dR antiL Hdagger, dimension seven,
B=1,L=-1. Actual dual gauge modules, SL(2,C) Weyl invariance and identical-field
exterior statistics are retained. The LR covariant seed has the required
identical-Q half. Substitution of the neutral Hdagger VEV produces the selected
LEFT udd–anti-neutrino components, dimension six; full SM invariance is not
asserted after EW substitution.

Keep the neutral mixing mass M separate from the scalar Yukawa vertex y.
With v=〈chi0〉, m=m_chid², Dirac Yukawa Y and Hdagger VEV w, the scalar prefactor
in the exact mu=0 chart is

\[
 k_X w=-\frac{C_X v y\,\overline Y w}{\Lambda m\overline M},
 \qquad d=Y\overline w.
\]

The full neutral mass kernel is the line (1,0,-d/M), not an assumed arbitrary
light projection. Its barred xi coordinate is star(-d/M). The actual four-
fermion coefficient is nonzero precisely when w,v,y,Y are nonzero and at least
one of C_L,C_R is nonzero, on the domain Lambda,m,M nonzero. A concrete exact
point with all parameters one and M=y*star(v) is certified. The two chirality
sectors have independent probes. Tensor independence says nothing about
interference in a particular decay amplitude.

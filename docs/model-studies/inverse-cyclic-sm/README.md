# A Constructed Algebraic Lepton-Parity SM Input

A bounded inverse charge filter selects Z2 lepton parity: quark species are
even, lepton species odd, and the Higgs neutral. The Lean construction has
three standard SM generations, a charged singlet spectator pair with an
explicit invariant nonzero mass, and precisely the SM fermions as its mass
radical. Each charged-fermion Yukawa channel has a nonzero gauge/Lorentz/
residual-invariant exterior coefficient. All derivative-free absolute B1
coefficients on the declared signed SM/Higgs alphabet vanish under the
imposed action.

This is a classical algebraic input, not an anomaly-free UV theory or an exact
quantum protection certificate. Lepton parity is familiar; no new physical
model is claimed. Its quantum validity, symmetry origin and simple-GUT lift
are outside the proved endpoint. Electroweak breaking, realistic masses,
matching and decay rates are not derived.

| Item | What is proved |
| --- | --- |
| Finite inverse filter | 649 Yukawa-parametrized charge pairs, 572 survivors, for orders 2 through 12; labeled tuples, not physical models |
| SM content | Explicit gauge-equivariant three-copy identifications for each of five all-left species |
| Mass | Constructed invariant spectator evaluation mass with exact SM radical |
| Yukawa | Nonzero physical exterior coefficients in Q uc H, Q dc H-dagger and L ec H-dagger channels |
| B1 target | All degrees on the stated derivative-free signed SM/Higgs coefficient domain |

The all-degree argument combines the even-fermion Lorentz constraint with
lepton parity, forcing the integer three-baryon charge to be even. It does
not conclude prohibition from charge neutrality or from checking four
operators. No all-degree claim is made about all 572 survivors, other operator alphabets,
anomalies or generated interactions.

The [bounded converse](../../topics/inverse-cyclic-sm-construction/bounded-converse-results.md)
subsequently proves that all 572 filtered assignments prohibit the actual
three-generation dimension-six DeltaL=1 four-family sector, while the other
77 admit a nonzero bounded coefficient. This classifies that bounded
predicate, not all-degree B1 protection or realized UV models.

The [three bounded follow-ups](../../topics/inverse-cyclic-sm-construction/three-followups-results.md)
add selected diagonal LFV iff tests and one-copy B2/LLHH properties. The 572
labeled assignments have 151 fixed-order unit orbits; 316 assignments forbid
both additional specified domains. Effective phase-image sizes are certified
without identifying UV models or proving all-degree protection.

The [converse-boundary continuation](../../topics/prohibition-converse-boundaries/results.md)
adds an all-degree criterion guarded by actual carrier support. The existing
lepton-parity construction also refutes necessity of detecting every raw B1
content: three Q fields are neutral but have no actual coefficient at odd
fermion degree. This does not classify all 572 assignments. The continuation's
copy/mass existence theorem concerns the separately fixed full-SM SU(5) family;
it does not supply a simple-GUT completion of this cyclic input.

The [support/LFV continuation](../../topics/sm-support-and-lfv-decomposition/results.md)
constructs the actual DUQL*(H-dagger H)^r coefficient family for every power,
with allowance iff 3q+l=0, and proves the signed three-generation fermion
exterior bound 180. These retain the neutral-Higgs input and do not classify
all contents or change the finite assignment counts. A complete all-degree
classification still needs the covariant spanning certificate in the
[boundary record](../../topics/sm-support-and-lfv-decomposition/support-boundary.md).
